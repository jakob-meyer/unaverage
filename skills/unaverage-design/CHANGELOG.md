# Changelog

## 3.4.3, 2026-11-02

Fixes for pages in Safari, found when the author opened a result there. The font fault was reproduced on two result pages in Safari's engine and is gone without the preload line. The blend fault comes from one screenshot. Not measured in a blind test.

- **Font preloads** (`fonts.md` 2, `review.md` 3, `scripts/measure.mjs`): HTML the user opens as a file has no font preload. Opened from disk, the preloaded font failed in Safari's engine on both pages tested, and Chrome still loaded it with only a console error. `measure.mjs` counts font preloads, and the new gate "Page in Safari" needs 0. Over a web server this is untested.
- **Blended images** (`images-and-assets.md` 11.2, `review.md` 3, `scripts/measure.mjs`): a blend is baked into the file instead of left to `mix-blend-mode`, and text stays above the image. `measure.mjs` counts blended images, wrappers and background images included.
- **Short window** (`review.md` 2): websites and landing pages also get a first-screen render at 1440 x 800, because a browser window on a laptop is lower than 900 px.
- **Safari in the wrap-up** (`review.md` 3): the checks run in Chrome, so the wrap-up names Safari as unchecked unless the page was opened there.
- **Font path** (`fonts.md`): the `@font-face` example uses a relative path, because a path from the root finds no file when the page is opened from disk.

## 3.4.0, 2026-10-31

Ask first, then directions. When the user has not shown what the look should come from, directions are no longer formed from the model's own world: the first message asks for things, and ranking and directions follow the answers. Tested on three web and product-UI briefs with three jury models. 3.4 and 3.3 never met in a pair: each stood blind against the same page by a strong model without the skill, in the same session. The rule fixed in advance on the change in that gap was met, at about half a point on the ten-point scale, with one run per brief and a simulated user who had a list of likes ready. Most of the gain came from one brief, one brief showed none, and distinctiveness rose while clarity fell. A sales-deck brief served as a harm check and showed no large harm; that says nothing about decks being better. Against a strong model without the skill and with the same background, 3.4 still loses on two of the three web and product briefs and on the sales deck. Social, carousel and brand work were not rebuilt with 3.4, and answers from real users are not tested yet.

- **Kickoff without directions** (SKILL.md, `dialogue.md` M1c): with the direction open, the goal clear and fewer than two things that show what the look should draw on, the first message is one numbered text block without a question tool: what was understood, the effort, five short questions and assumptions to correct. Ranking, candidates and seed follow once, after the answers, and the next message is the mockups. Empty answers change nothing about that and start no second round.
- **New questions Q16 to Q19** (`dialogue.md` 3.3): what customers hold or look at, things the user loves the look of from anywhere, what they tell a customer who hesitates, and what must not be promised or must be said. These and the trade-off pairs carry no default answer, so the user's words stay their own.
- **Named things in the world and the ranking** (`exploration.md` 2, `directions/README.md`, `dialogue.md` 4.2): things the user named come first, with the user as origin, and at least one mockup shows one. A named thing counts for the one direction whose anchor shows it best and is never "obvious" by origin. Objects, documents and places may carry anchor and form; brands, sites and works give only a principle. The name and the person behind the offer are always on the list.
- **Dose "quiet"** (`craft.md` 5.2): it limits extra means, not the anchor's type contrast or one color area from the project world. A value outside the corridor that comes only from those is recorded as intentional in field 9 and named in the wrap-up.
- **Consumer apps** (`directions/README.md` 2.1, `craft.md` 6.1, `product-design.md`): a second voice from the product's world is the default, for content names and key numbers, up to scale 5 and in any weight.
- **Review** (`review.md` R1, `templates/review-brief.md`): for screens, R1 also asks in the small view whether the page reads as a finished product or as a wireframe, and whether the mood contradicts the subject or what the user asked for.
- **Words before codes** (`copy.md` 6): the reader learns no code; initials and code numbers carry a word or a legend.
- **Contract and market module** (`templates/design-contract.md`, `markets/german.md`): things and words from the user go into field 6 word for word; the German module follows the new path and questions.

## 3.3.1, 2026-10-30

Deck rules and tool fixes after a blind round on the pitch-deck brief and a practice test with a talk deck built with 3.3. The deck rules apply to decks only. Tested in blind pairwise comparisons with three jury models: on the pitch-deck brief two runs ahead of 3.3 (20 of 24 verdicts, half a point to three quarters of a point on the ten-point scale), level with 3.1; on an unseen sales-deck brief ahead of 3.3 with the tool fixes (10 of 12 verdicts, about one point). Against a strong model without the skill the sales deck still loses. The tool fixes were measured on fixtures, the test decks and, for the web, against 3.3 on the 128 calibration pages.

- **Pitch deck directions** (`startup.md` A1): EDI as the edge direction counts its material by the gate (the company's own photos, client material or a public-domain archive on the subject); ONE only on title, chapter, vision and closing slides, never as the base of the proof slides; when the ranking would recommend ONE as the base, the strongest direction with material is recommended with ONE as its accent. Without the company's own photos, one public-domain archive search before the ranking, unless the user chose no photos.
- **Proof and closing slides** (`craft.md` 12.2): a base that is a comparison, a period or a whole is drawn at true scale; traction shows the earlier value next to the latest when both are given; a price slide may show the amount per typical customer with its working; the ask shows only the given parts of the use of funds; no template stat slide (a lone figure on an empty or dark ground without the deck's forms).
- **Drawings in decks** (`craft.md` 12.2): every drawing meets the quality rule of the product drawing, in one technique; without a direction's own illustration technique, objects and places are drawn as orthographic views, maps, plans and data forms; people, animals and plants as a slide's main image come from photos, a public-domain archive or a stand-in photo; each drawing is checked at full size and at 360 px.
- **Sample case in a deck** (`craft.md` 12.2, 5.1; SKILL.md, Limits): introduced once by an "Example" label in label type where the case begins, never on the title slide; "made up" goes to the speaker notes and the review view.
- **Ledger as a file in decks** (`craft.md` 12.1): `ledger.md` before the first render, with each value's slides, its states and the same statement in words; the slides' words are read against it before each review round and after every fix.
- **Deck delivery** (SKILL.md, Curated delivery): HTML decks are built on the deck shell and also ship as a PDF with one page per slide.

- **Talk deck gate** (generic check H4): with `--deck talk` every content text needs 40 px at 1080 height, not only the most frequent size. A content text is a block, its words summed across inline markup, word-by-word builds and line breaks; from 3 words below 40 px it is red, with 1 or 2 words yellow. Sources, labels, captions and page numbers marked with class `source` or `label` (also `caption`, `footnote`, `credit`, `src`, `data-role`, `figcaption`, `cite`, `small`, a `sup` footnote marker) may go down to 18 px. Slides are measured in the stage's own layout px, so a viewer that scales the 1920 x 1080 stage to the window no longer lowers the targets, in talk and reading decks; SVG text counts at its rendered size, in reading decks too. A hidden or empty surface is skipped with a warning instead of aborting the check (`generic-catalog.md` H4).
- **Stat banner** (D4): scale and step rows no longer count (one unit, constant step left to right: 1 2 3, 01 02 03, 2024 2025 2026, 0% 25% 50%, also an axis with the unit on one end item, 0 25 50 75 100%; with a unit or sign from 4 items or with 0 at either end) and are named in the value; a number needs a digit, and decimal axes such as 0.000 0.250 0.500 are read as decimals.
- **PDF** (`scripts/pdf.mjs`, new): one page per slide or card at its size on screen, without the viewer around it, builds and reveals in their end state; documents with their own `@page` rules; page count and page sizes read back from the PDF, with warnings (`scripts/README.md`; `use-cases/README.md` 5).
- **Deck shell** (`templates/deck-shell.html`, new): structure for HTML decks, not a look: fixed 1920 x 1080 slides that fit the window width on screen (the scripts measure and capture them at 1920 x 1080), presentation mode (F or a double click, arrow keys, click zones, Esc), builds with `data-step`, speaker notes, print rules and the classes `source` and `label`; its demo slides are replaced (`craft.md` 12.2).
- **Direction choice** (`directions/README.md` 11.5): a direction that fills a missing class may share the group or the map position with a chosen one, not both (wording only, as in the German source).
- **Windows:** the browser lookup also finds Edge under Program Files and per-user installs of Chrome, Edge and Brave under `LOCALAPPDATA`.

## 3.3.0, 2026-10-23

Truth, review and image rules after a second round of web tests. An intermediate version 3.2 was tested against 3.1 in blind pairwise comparisons and not adopted; 3.3 builds on 3.1 and takes from 3.2 the value ledger, the full-page review views with their script fixes, the honest headings and the core of the sample-case and brief-feature rules. Tested in blind pairwise comparisons on seven web, product UI and deck briefs, including an unseen landing page brief: level with 3.1 within run-to-run noise, weaker on the pitch deck.

- **Sample case:** on persuasion surfaces one made-up case runs through in steps, the product's outputs within the brief's ranges, introduced once where it begins by one line that reviewers do not flag; never an invented outcome or condition, and an assumed tax, fee or term stays open until the user confirms it (`craft.md` 5.1, point 9; SKILL.md, Limits; `review.md` 8; `dialogue.md` 3.4).
- **Brief features** as a control, state or result at their place, plus the category's frame controls; long product views folded at 390 px; planning details at text size (`craft.md` 5.1, 11; `product-design.md` C1; `STI.md` 8).
- **Value ledger:** repeated sample values match, the before state is marked, a chart marks what its number counts (`craft.md` 9.2, 12.1; `review.md` 19).
- **Reviewers see the whole page** in parts and two mobile screens; question 6 asks about apparent records and contradictions; a missing fact becomes an asset request. `render.mjs` loads lazy content, adds `--reduced-motion` and cuts tall elements from one full-page capture; `pixels.py slice` takes `--max` (`review.md` 8, 9, 15, 18; review brief).
- **Honest headings** in decision-critical sections, and a default answer for Q15 (`copy.md` 1, point 10; `dialogue.md` 3.3; `review.md` 19).
- **Late fixes:** after the last reviewer call only local fixes that align with what exists, then the gates again; larger open flaws go to the user with the cost of one more round, bookable as an add-on; without an answer the work is accepted (`review.md` 13, 16; `dialogue.md` M5, M5s).
- **Image source as the user's choice** (Q5): none, free photos, or the user's own, sent or from their website after the user confirms rights and consent; the recommendation first, with what the others lose; on websites a free photo never carries the hero alone unless asked (`images-and-assets.md` 2; `dialogue.md` 3.3; contract field 7; `credits.md` 3; `eu-dach-compliance.md` 9, 12).

## History of the German edition (design-craft), summarized

- **2.5.2, 2026-10-15:** first-reach props (a stamp, a highlighter, a calendar entry or a sticky note is a cliché while it only shows the topic); the signature element and prop rotate across brands; a rebuilt real interface follows its reference capture; every text in the image goes through the language check; in the feed the caption adds what the image does not show; reviewers judge type size, spacing and contrast only at full size or by measurement; the contract lists all brand colors with roles. Not tested on its own.
- **2.5.1, 2026-10-10:** scope follows the brief; the core function of a brief is visible at a glance. Not tested on its own.
- **2.5.0, 2026-10-10:** feeling before category in feed headlines; outsourcing ("link in bio") never replaces content; illustration only with its own handwriting; story copy introduces no new facts without a source.
- **2.4.0, 2026-10-10:** open items in final form, no hedges on the image, no compositing into foreign photos, fewer repeats, rhythm in calm directions, the product recognizable as what it is, strong hero headlines, mandatory notices only with their trigger, E6 extended.
- **2.3.0, 2026-10-10:** the hybrid: the generative core of version 1 (one tangible idea from the product's world, material, the product shown as built, benefit-led copy) with the knowledge of version 2 and a smaller reviewer budget; replace, don't delete.
- **2.2.0, 2026-10-09:** proof on the key surface, stand-in quality, rhythm in sequences, presentation view without gap markers (new tell E6, calibrated without false alarms on 75 human sites).
- **2.1.0, 2026-10-09:** impact first, honest but never empty (best-form stand-ins, drafted story copy), two views, building impact, review capped, target-market spelling gate, visual tell V26.
- **2.0.0, 2026-10-08:** from a craft layer to a full design skill: eight stages, ranked directions at three distances, effort levels, 13 directions with datasheets, five registers, four modules, 55 use-case profiles, independent reviewers R1 to R4, generic check v2 calibrated on 53 top references and 38 real AI pages, references on craft, fonts, images, accessibility and German law, scripts.
- **1.2.0, 2026-10-05:** through-line for sequences; images with watermarks or unclear origin are discarded.
- **1.1.0, 2026-10-05:** collecting improvements to the skill.
- **1.0.0, 2026-10-05:** first release: a five-step process, eight craft levers, image sourcing, quick check, hand-drawn accents.

Six rounds of internal blind tests against version 1 (German briefs, AI judges) shaped 2.1 to 2.5; method, results and limits are described in the repository's test documentation.
