# Origin and use

unaverage-design is a design skill built and tested in Claude Code; it also runs in the Claude app and in Codex. Author: Jakob Meyer. Before its first public release it was called design-craft.

## Origin

Version 1 came from the analysis of a publicly shared comparison of two pitch decks with identical content (one generated by AI in a single pass, one designed by a professional, shared on LinkedIn on 2026-10-05). Version 2 rests on dedicated groundwork in October 2026: research into design history, studios, product and web design, marketing impact, presentation and information design, AI genericness, craft and resources, process and quality, use cases, law and accessibility, plus about 70 rendered and measured reference works. Sources are listed at the end of the respective file in `references/`. Version 3 is the English edition, adapted from the German 2.5.2; rules for specific markets live in `references/markets/`.

## What the skill contains and what it does not

- The skill takes only abstract principles and measured values from its references. It contains no designs, texts or images from third-party works.
- Names of studios, brands and works serve only as evidence, never as templates. Signatures of living studios and artists are not reproduced.
- Fonts are not included. The catalog lists only freely licensed typefaces; license and source are in `references/fonts.md`.
- The accents in a hand-drawn style in `assets/doodles/` (SVG) were created specifically for this skill.
- The scripts in `scripts/` need `puppeteer-core` (Apache-2.0). It is not included; `package.json` and `package-lock.json` in this folder pin the version, and `npm install` in this folder installs it.

## Legal notes

The notes on law and accessibility in `references/accessibility.md`, `references/compliance.md`, the market modules in `references/markets/` and the other references are checklists for designers, current as of October 2026, without warranty. They do not replace legal advice.
