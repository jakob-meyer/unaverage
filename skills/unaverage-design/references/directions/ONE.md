# ONE The One Idea

**In one line:** A single visual idea says the message in one image, with wit and few means; it is readable in three seconds, and the brand sits inside the idea instead of in the logo.

**Group** IV Idea and system | **Lead effect** W14 Punchline (also W3, W9, W11, W13) | **Loudness** medium (span 1 to 4 of 6) | **Material** no outside material, but an idea from name, product or result

How to read this, ground rules G1 to G14, registers and combination rules: [README.md](README.md).

## 1 Effect

- Clever, surprising, confident, memorable, shareable. Audience: "Understood in one second, and I have to grin." (H)
- Creative is, next to reach, the biggest advertising lever (47% of sales lift according to Nielsen, 49% of brand impact according to Kantar; medium). 76.7% of 1,700 B2B spots reach only one star at System1: neutrality is the most common reaction, and the punchline works against it (medium).
- Risk: the idea has to be explained; a clip-art metaphor instead of an idea; the logo larger than the punchline.

## 3 Principle and evidence

- **School:** Paul Rand (IBM, Eye-Bee-M, NeXT with a complete derivation [1]), Saul Bass (title sequences [2]), Vignelli (high).
- **Motif from the subject:** Windows 2012 (the name as a window), Burger King 2021 (the name between two buns, Cannes Gold [3]), Heinz Draw Ketchup (an existing asset as the idea, Creative Effectiveness Grand Prix 2024 [4]); the Cannes Design Grand Prix 2018 to 2025 went almost only to functional ideas; the glance test of three seconds (Duarte) (all high).
- **References (inside the corridor, measured on the works, not on the host pages):** Reference: Shot on iPhone (result instead of product, one gray sender line, text never over the photo; Cannes Grand Prix 2015 and 2025, high [5]). Reference: Mailchimp "Did You Mean MailChimp?" (the name as an asset in mock brands, riddle before resolution; medium). Reference: Apple Keynote (the screen shows the object of thought, build-up in single steps, the punchline as the only color; high for the reception). Reference: Oatly "Help Dad" and posters 2025 (two reading levels, mirror symmetry for two parties, footnotes as proof; high [6]). Reference: Spotify Wrapped (one card, one statement; high [7]). Reference: Expedia carousel (one giant word per slide; medium).

## 4 Parameters

**Typography**

- Classes: one typeface that does not contradict the idea (neutral or geometric sans or classic serif); often one weight in two sizes; a wordmark as the image is allowed.
- Scale per surface: two to three sizes; the level-1 element (word or number) from 15% of the format height; secondary elements a factor of 2.5 to 4 smaller. No minimum type scale applies to the page.
- Weights and tracking: one to two weights; display 0 to -0.02 em.
- Setting: level 1 at most 7 words (poster 3 to 6, keynote 1 to 7 per state); the sender line small and always the same; level 2 (body text, footnote) only with dwell time; no subline that explains the idea; riddle before resolution when the mechanism demands it.
- Pairing A: League Spartan 600 to 800 for level 1, 400 for the sender line ([fonts.md](../fonts.md), ONE).
- Pairing B: Castoro 400 and Italic for level 1, Hanken Grotesk 400 for the sender ([fonts.md](../fonts.md), ONE).
- Pairing C: Epilogue 700 to 800 as the sole voice in two sizes ([fonts.md](../fonts.md), ONE).

**Color**

- Logic: one to three solid colors plus black and white, or color exclusively from the photo; the brand color as an area only when it is itself the idea (reference: Mailchimp 2018, yellow); a neutral carrier in black or white for photo ideas; in a step build-up the punchline is the only color of the last state.
- Roles: field (solid color or photo) 70 to 90%; idea 10 to 25%; text 2 to 5%.
- Saturation strategy: word and symbol punchline A or B with a narrower value (up to 15%); photo idea A on the surface, color only from the photo (measured up to 30%); solid-color idea C with at most two hue ranges.
- Starter palette (placeholder; the brand color replaces the signal, recompute contrasts):

| Variant | Role and hex | Contrast (WCAG) |
|---|---|---|
| A typographic | white #FFFFFF; black #111111; signal #D42E10 | black 18.88:1; signal 5.00:1 on white, white on it 5.00:1; black on signal only 3.77:1, so large type only |
| B full-bleed surface | yellow #FFD400 with black #111111 | 13.19:1 |

**Layout:** one element per surface; the axis from the idea (asymmetric, or deliberately centered when the punchline is symmetric); very low density with two reading levels: level 1 in three seconds, level 2 as a reward; much emptiness for a graphic idea; text never over the main photo, but in a band, margin or field.

**Imagery:** symbol, rebus, paper cutout, pictogram, result instead of product, user photo with attribution and rights; cut edges instead of vector perfection; no stock metaphors (light bulb, rocket, handshake, puzzle, target, chess piece). Cheapest real route: a cutout of an everyday object from the project (about 20 minutes, [images-and-assets.md](../images-and-assets.md)).

**Form:** basic shapes only with meaning in the idea; cut edge; lines only as part of the idea; no decoration, no containers, no shadows; every form has a job in the reading path.

**Signature elements (at least one):**

1. Rebus or word picture from the name (principle Eye-Bee-M).
2. Result instead of product plus an always identical sender line (reference: Shot on iPhone).
3. An existing asset becomes the idea: form, name, color (reference: Heinz, Burger King).
4. Design that does something: a functional idea instead of ornament.
5. Mirror symmetry when the message has two parties (reference: Oatly "Help Dad").
6. Step build-up to the punchline, the punchline in the only color (reference: Apple Keynote).
7. Riddle series with a late sender (reference: Mailchimp "Did You Mean MailChimp?").
8. Image equation: old, arrow, new.

**Motion:** one beat to the punchline: build-up in sync with the spoken sentence, one element per change, text beats 0.5 to 1.5 s, cuts in rhythm (Bass principle), ending on a still frame; the brand in the first two seconds, integrated instead of as a logo plate; moving image goes to a video skill.

**Data:** a number as the image only when it is itself the punchline, with a source for third-party numbers ([craft.md](../craft.md) 9.2); a position matrix without values only for order, never for quantities; proof surfaces following DAT.

**Iconography:** no icons; a symbol only when it is the idea, drawn specifically.

## 6 Dose

| Level | What changes |
|---|---|
| quiet | a typographic punchline or symbol in one color on white, sender line (Rand principle) |
| medium | a photo idea with a constant sender line as a series, or a punchline with one solid color and an image metaphor (reference: Shot on iPhone) |
| loud | solid color as the idea, riddle series with a late sender, a long second reading level, a full-bleed poster campaign (reference: Did You Mean MailChimp, Oatly) |

## 7 AI trap

- **AI proximity:** medium (a warning, not a ranking criterion).
- **Caricature:** clip-art metaphors (light bulb, rocket, puzzle, target, handshake, chess piece); a mid-century palette of mustard, petrol and orange with torn paper and movie-poster silhouettes, but without an idea; a rebus without meaning; a pun without an image; a subline that explains the idea; the logo larger than the punchline; a headline with a black gradient over a stock photo; generated surreal images without a connection to the subject.
- **Mechanism:** trait instead of principle; what gets copied is the surface of American modernism, not the idea.
- **Review question (also for R1 and R3, before the measurement findings):** Can the idea be described in one sentence, is the brand recognizable without the logo, and would the idea not work for a competitor?
- **Countermeasure:** the source of the idea in the subject (name taken literally, physical form, material, result, user ritual; idea angles in [exploration.md](../exploration.md)); internally generate at least five ideas in parallel and condense them with four questions: connection to the purpose, complexity, similarity to existing work, overtaken by a better idea (johnson banks for Mozilla [8]; parallel drafts work, high); block standard symbols.
- **Watch list:** tell E1, E5, C8; tell D7 only as a note; tell A2 is suspended for a single-word surface; visual V1, V9, V19, V21 ([generic-catalog.md](../generic-catalog.md)).

## 8 Media notes by register

| Register | What differs |
|---|---|
| Operate | at most one punchline in the empty state, the success moment or on the error page; otherwise the Operate register |
| Persuade | a campaign microsite like a poster (one element on a full-bleed surface), OG image, logo; display ads in all sizes, check the smallest first; after that, order from the base |
| Read | only cover, chapter dividers, title and closing slide; never proof slides |
| Feed | hook tile, single-image punchline, thumbnail, riddle series, story with the punchline at the end; level 1 in the safe area; sender from 24 px at 1080 px width; Reels and paid social in the platform-native feed mode |
| Distance | keynote: main word about 18 to 19% of the height (one-word slides from 160 px at 1080, smallest content 40 px), chapters as a single digit, step build-up; poster and trade fair following the long-distance mode, test at 10% size |
