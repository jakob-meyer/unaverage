# HER Heritage and Quotation

**In one line:** A form from the brand's own past, or from an era with a real connection to the brand, is uncovered and set in the present; the quotation occupies exactly one axis, everything else is of today.

**Group** II Value and origin | **Lead effect** W15 Provenance (also W1, W5, W6, W13) | **Loudness** medium (span 2 to 4 of 6) | **Material** heritage inventory or an archive with a real connection to the brand; without it only a dated era quotation with a reason

How to read this, ground rules G1 to G14, registers and combination rules: [README.md](README.md).

## 1 Effect

- Rooted, credible, unmistakable, self-assured. Audience: "They really exist, and they know where they come from." (H)
- Existing assets carry recognition: over 80% recognize the Mastercard circles without the name (medium [1][2]). Origin works as proof, not as a claim.
- Risk: retro kitsch, invented heritage, heritage with an expiry date when the audience changes (counterexample: Robinhood replaced its award-winning 2020 identity in 2024).

## 3 Principle and evidence

- **Heritage reduction:** uncover the brand's best-known element, enlarge it, leave out the rest. Mastercard (Pentagram 2016, 2019), Burger King (JKR 2021, Cannes Gold), Nike Run (Porto Rocha, Volt orange from the shoebox, D&AD Yellow Pencil 2025) (high [1][3][4]).
- **Era quotation:** postmodernism, Googie, Frutiger Aero only as a dated quotation with a connection to the product or audience, following M-TRD in [modules.md](modules.md) (medium).
- **Archive:** public-domain collections (Smithsonian, Met, Art Institute of Chicago); reproductions of public-domain two-dimensional works are not newly protected (high [5]; EU: no new protection for faithful reproductions; Germany: [markets/eu-dach-compliance.md](../markets/eu-dach-compliance.md) 12).
- **References for the base (inside the corridor):** Reference: Scout Motors (the new vehicle against the original in the same crop, archive as numbered prints; Awwwards 2025, high [6]). Reference: Graza (seventies wordmark as a surface, colors from the label; D&AD 2023, high [7]). Reference: Faculty (heritage serif, wordmark as a stone relief; D&AD Wood Pencil 2025, medium [8]; measured on work images and the case page).
- **References as an accent (inside the corridor, mode quiet and medium):** Reference: Siemens (founder portrait in present-day colors on ORD; Red Dot and iF 2024 for the brand design). Reference: Nothing (dot matrix from a real product surface on RUL).

## 4 Parameters

**Typography**

- Classes: revival serif or historical sans for the quotation, a contemporary sans for explanation and operation; or a display from the real product history (dot matrix, housing print). Never three eras. Strongest option: have the house typeface digitized from the brand's own archive (clear the rights).
- Scale: quiet and medium 2 to 5, loud 7 to 20, with one poster moment or closing band beyond that range; deck 3 to 5 (section 5).
- Weights: quotation in the historical weight (usually Regular), explanatory text 400.
- Tracking: follow the original, not today's fashion; explanatory text 0.
- Setting: explanatory text contemporary and brief; archive images with year; image credit following [templates/credits.md](../../templates/credits.md) 4 (collected in a deck, in print also at the image edge); mono only for data (coordinates, founding year, legal line); chapter words like place-name signs.
- Pairing A: Libre Caslon Display 400 for the quotation, Libre Franklin 400 for explanation; no tabular figures ([fonts.md](../fonts.md), HER).
- Pairing B: Linden Hill Regular and Italic for titles, Albert Sans 400 for text; old-style figures suit the quotation, not data ([fonts.md](../fonts.md), HER).
- Pairing C: Handjet only for names, figures, navigation, Funnel Sans 400 for everything else ([fonts.md](../fonts.md), HER).

**Color**

- Logic: palette from archive or era (label, paintwork, print), filtered through the present: same tones, today's quantities. Signal color bound to the purchase action.
- Roles: archive or paper ground 40 to 60%; ink 10 to 20%; era colors 10 to 30%; action color under 1% of the area.
- Saturation strategy A or B up to 25%: surface without image up to 10%, with an archive, label or campaign image up to 25%; a single closing band may be strategy C. Record it in the design contract.
- Starter palette (placeholder; the brand color replaces the signal, recompute contrasts):

|---|---|---|
| Archive | paper gray #E9E6DF (only when tied to an archive, otherwise tell B3); ink #222222; navy #1D2B45; archive red #A63A2B | ink 12.77:1, navy 11.36:1, archive red 5.17:1 on paper gray; white on navy 14.15:1, on archive red 6.44:1 |
| Retro tech | black #0A0A0A; aluminum #C9CCD1; display red #E6332A | aluminum 12.29:1; display red 4.59:1 on black (borderline for text, better from 24 px) |

**Layout:** a contemporary grid as the frame; the quotation sits in a defined place (logo, title, image pair, pattern). Density medium; archive as numbered prints, ring or timeline (a real sequence, then tell A6 is not a flaw). Image pairs with a shared axis and the same crop. Old and new in the same crop instead of a paragraph "Since 19xx".

**Imagery:** archive photos, originals, construction drawings, labels, advertising of the time; public-domain museum holdings with a motif from the project world, treated alike as a series (duotone or line); present-day photos in the same light and crop; paper grain and halftone screens only from real scans. Rights: terms of protection, consent of the people shown also for archive photos ([images-and-assets.md](../images-and-assets.md), [compliance.md](../compliance.md) 3; EU and DACH: [markets/eu-dach-compliance.md](../markets/eu-dach-compliance.md) 9 and 12). Cheapest real route: scan or photograph the user's old printed matter, labels, photos (about 30 minutes); substitute: a public-domain archive on the topic.

**Form:** from the heritage (circles, coat-of-arms shapes without pathos, paintwork lines, scale marks, the product's light bar); radii as one family from the heritage; lines as a hairline or dashed as in print; depth from image pairs, not from shadows; containers as a print, label or passe-partout; no patina filters.

**Signature elements (at least one):**

1. Old against new in the same crop (reference: Scout Motors).
2. Heritage reduction: uncover and enlarge the best-known element (reference: Mastercard).
3. Historical person or view in present-day colors (reference: Siemens).
4. Display from a real product surface of the past (reference: Nothing).
5. Archive ring or numbered prints as navigation through the history.
6. Material from the history as the wordmark (reference: Faculty, relief).
7. Label or packaging as a page (reference: Graza).

**Motion:** crossfade old to new; film grain only from real material; archive motion (flip book, cuts in the historical rhythm); otherwise calm, endless loops pausable.

**Data:** timelines with real dates; years set typographically large; a present-day metric against a historical baseline; every year sourced (tell E5).

**Iconography:** from historical pictograms or signs of the archive, redrawn; no vintage badges from templates; standard sets only for functions.

## 6 Dose

| Level | What changes |
|---|---|
| quiet | one heritage element (logo detail, color, letterform) in an otherwise contemporary system (reference: Mastercard, Nothing) |
| medium | image pairs old and new, archive as its own chapter, era typeface for titles (reference: Siemens, Faculty) |
| loud | archive and present as a continuous story, chapter words, era palette as areas, wordmark as a surface (reference: Scout Motors, Graza) |

## 7 AI trap

- **AI proximity:** medium (a warning, not a ranking criterion).
- **Caricature:** a "Since 1890" coat of arms without basis; vintage badges; sepia and scratch filters; three retro typefaces; chrome 3D type, butterflies and glitter as a Y2K costume; a mid-century palette not tied to the brand; retro mono as a tech costume; cream as paper without paper.
- **Mechanism:** heritage as mood instead of proof; missing material is replaced by stylistic devices.
- **Review question (also for R1 and R3, before the measurement findings):** Which one element demonstrably comes from the history of this brand or its subject, and what of it is set in the present?
- **Countermeasure:** heritage inventory before the design (logos, colors, forms, characters, archive holdings, sorted by recognition; [exploration.md](../exploration.md)); without an inventory only a dated era quotation with a reason (M-TRD); the present in grid, operation and explanatory text.
- **Watch list:** tell A5, tell B3 (cream not tied to paper or a label), tell C1, tell E5 (invented years); visual V9, V11, V25 ([generic-catalog.md](../generic-catalog.md)).

## 8 Media notes by register

| Register | What differs |
|---|---|
| Operate | quotation only in names, start screen or display elements (dot matrix for status); everything else following the Operate register |
| Persuade | product with an origin chapter, archive as navigation, closing image with the main action; packaging: label as a page, mandatory notices legible |
| Read | chronicle, anniversary report, brand presentation with image pairs and the year as chapter; archive images with year, credits collected following [templates/credits.md](../../templates/credits.md) 4; in the investor deck heritage only as proof (market position, customer base) |
| Feed | series "then and now" with a fixed frame; archive find as a recurring format; quotation and sender in the safe area |
| Distance | one heritage element large (wordmark, form, color) or the year as a large form; archive detail only for close viewing |
