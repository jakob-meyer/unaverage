# TYP Type as Image

**In one line:** Type becomes the main image, oversized, stacked, cropped or broken against a recognizable grid; a small function layer with real data keeps the spectacle readable.

**Group** V Expression | **Lead effect** W11 Talkability (also W7, W12, W13, W14; against W4) | **Loudness** loud (span 4 to 6 of 6) | **Material** no outside material, but a typeface with character, a wordmark or a house typeface

How to read this, ground rules G1 to G14, registers and combination rules: [README.md](README.md).

## 1 Effect

- Energetic, self-assured, cultural, contemporary, loud without color. Audience: "That sticks." (H) It stops the scroll; culture-minded and design-minded young audiences read it as stance. Paula Scher's Public Theater style changed New York theater advertising [1] (high).
- Risk: legibility and accessibility. On the award-winning experimental sites, accessibility is the weakest or second-weakest jury score (6.60 to 7.40); two of three rendered winner sites first showed only a loading screen (medium).

## 3 Principle and evidence

The break needs a counterpart: first the grid, then the deviation. Large type carries the image, small type carries the facts (medium).

- **School:** Wolfgang Weingart (Basel from the early 1970s: stepped, letterspaced, layered typography), April Greiman ("Does It Make Sense?", 1986 [2]), Paula Scher (Public Theater 1994 [1]), Tschichold and Moholy-Nagy for the typophoto, Saul Bass for moving type (high); David Carson (weak evidence).
- **References inside the corridor:** Reference: Scout Motors (chapter words of 270 px as place-name signs, a closing band at 504 px, mono only for data; Awwwards E-Commerce of the Year 2025, high [3]). Reference: Lusion (Regular at 144 px, line height 1.0, registration marks at column edges, one breakout instead of twenty; Awwwards Site of the Year 2023, high). Reference: Lando Norris (serif and sans in the same sentence, display 110 px at line height 0.83; Awwwards Site of the Year 2025, high [4]). Reference: Wise (a caps house typeface in 900 only for stance and numbers; high for the identity). Reference: Helsing (the category name as an edge-to-edge caps line with about 140 px cap height; medium, own measurement, design without an award). Reference: Igloo (the opposite pole with a flat scale of about 1.2 and hierarchy through position; at the same time a warning case with 0 characters in the DOM).

## 4 Parameters

**Typography**

- Classes: expressive libre display (Velvetyne, Collletttivo) or a narrow or wide display sans with axes; a neutral text typeface for the function layer; two voices in the same sentence allowed (at most one to three words in the second voice). Display from L1 or L2 only with exactly one deliberate axis (G5); otherwise a scale from 5, line height up to 1.0 and weight from 700 produce the cluster Template display.
- Scale and largest type: polar, from 6.5, or up to 1.5 with hierarchy through position; never the template headline of 48 to 64 px as the largest type. Desktop from 100 px, slide from 160 px at 1080 px height, social from 15% of the format height.
- Weights: Regular at giant size (Lusion 400 at 144 px) or Black in caps (Wise 900); at most two weights in the display.
- Tracking and line height: display -0.02 to -0.04 em, caps display 0 to +0.02 em (below -0.04 tell A7 fires); line height 0.8 to 1.0, descenders do not touch.
- Function layer: 11 to 16 px, caps or mono, with real data (place, year, performance, coordinate) in fixed positions. From 11 px only for text nobody has to read, and with 4.5:1; text that must be read at 12 px or more (minimum type size in [use-cases/README.md](../use-cases/README.md) 4).
- Setting: chapter words replace eyebrow plus subheading; level 1 at most 3 to 7 words, as a hero in the Persuade register on desktop in at most four lines ([craft.md](../craft.md) 5.1, point 2); crop only when the statement stays readable; break long words in display deliberately, by hyphenation or by hand (G10; German compounds: [markets/german.md](../markets/german.md) 3).
- Pairing A: League Gothic (wdth 75 to 100) for chapter words and stacked setting, Epilogue 400 for text, Martian Mono for the function layer ([fonts.md](../fonts.md), TYP).
- Pairing B (two-voice setting): Fanwood Text Italic for emotional words, Archivo wdth 125 in 700 for facts, Sometype Mono for the function layer ([fonts.md](../fonts.md), TYP).
- Pairing C (culture, music): Le Murmure or Sporting Grotesque as display, Ronzino as text; OFL with Reserved Font Name, do not subset ([fonts.md](../fonts.md), TYP).

**Color**

- Logic: black and white plus one solid color; loudness through size, not through saturation; the signal color belongs to the main action; otherwise color only from scene images.
- Roles: type 25 to 50% of the area, ground 40 to 70%, solid color at most 15%.
- Saturation strategy (G4): B with a narrower value up to 15% on the surface; A when all color comes from scene images (Helsing); a single scene image or band up to 60%.
- Starter palette (placeholder; recompute contrasts after a change):

| Role | Hex | Contrast (WCAG) |
|---|---|---|
| Black | #0D0D0D | on white 19.44:1 |
| Paper | #F4F4F0 | black on it 17.63:1; does not count as cream (tell B3) |
| Solid vermilion | #FF3B00 | black on it 5.44:1; paper on it only 3.24:1, so large type only |

**Layout:** a grid exists, then gets broken; the break needs a recognizable counterpart. Density low to high, 10 to 40 visible words in the first screen; type as a surface; text zones over images only with contrast protection; words cropped at the edge; a fixed, calm main action in a corner (Scout, Lando); a closing image with the main action in the largest size.

**Imagery:** the key surface shows a tangible image idea from the product's world and the offer or its use as a proof image (cutout, photo, interface); the type accompanies or frames it, it never replaces it (SKILL.md stage 4, point 1). Typophoto (image and text as one statement); photos as a surface behind giant type. Imageless only on explicit request in the contract ([craft.md](../craft.md) 5.1 point 9).

**Form:** bars, lines, registration marks at column edges (Lusion); a grid as ornament only when it orders (otherwise tell C11); radius 0 or one pill form for controls.

**Signature elements (at least one):**

1. A chapter word over 200 px as a place-name sign in the image (reference: Scout Motors).
2. A closing band of a single word with the main action (reference: Scout Motors).
3. An edge-to-edge caps line of the category (reference: Helsing).
4. Two-voice setting: emotional words in serif, facts in sans (reference: Lando Norris).
5. A function layer with coordinates, date, performance data.
6. Staircase and stacked setting against a visible grid (Weingart principle).
7. Display in Regular at giant size instead of Black (reference: Lusion).

**Motion:** kinetic typography word by word, text beats 0.5 to 1.5 s, variable axes animated, size coupled to scroll; text always in the DOM; a full static version with reduced motion; no loading screen, no scroll hijacking.

**Data:** one number as a typographic monument, with a source for third-party numbers ([craft.md](../craft.md) 9.2); no charts.

**Iconography:** none; arrows and signs set typographically.

## 6 Dose

| Level | What changes |
|---|---|
| quiet | one oversized line per page, the rest calm in the base direction (reference: Helsing; Wise stance sentences) |
| medium | chapter words, two-voice sentences, function layer, closing band (reference: Scout Motors, Lando Norris, Lusion) |
| loud | stack, break, overlap, kinetic (Weingart and Scher principle, poster) |

## 7 AI trap

- **AI proximity:** medium (a warning, not a ranking criterion).
- **Caricature:** glitch, a torn-paper overlay, randomly rotated words, grunge texture, outline type, marquees without meaning; or the 72 px bold headline with a gradient word (cluster Template display); Bebas Neue or Anton as a caps poster from templates.
- **Mechanism:** the break has nothing to break against.
- **Review question (also for R1 and R3, before the measurement findings):** Is the grid that is broken against recognizable, does the key message stay readable in one second, and is every word in the DOM?
- **Countermeasure:** set the grid before the break; a function layer with real data; text in the DOM; reduced motion as a full version; measure contrast at the least favorable point; the display voice from the project world (wordmark, house typeface, an era with a connection to the project), not from L1 or L2.
- **Watch list:** tell A1, A3, A7, A8, C2, C11, D10, H4, H5; cluster Template display; visual V10, V22, V24 ([generic-catalog.md](../generic-catalog.md)).

## 8 Media notes by register

| Register | What differs |
|---|---|
| Operate | do not recommend; at most an onboarding title or a success moment in the display voice |
| Persuade | campaign, launch, event, studio; the main action fixed and calm in a corner; no loading curtain; a closing image instead of a link dump; on mobile rebreak chapter words instead of shrinking them (G11) |
| Read | only cover, chapter dividers, title and closing slide; content surfaces following the base direction |
| Feed | hooks and posters 4:5 and 9:16, the level-1 word in the safe area; kinetics with text beats; reduction test at 360 px width |
| Distance | poster, trade fair, keynote: cap height by reading distance ([README.md](README.md) 2.3), one-word slides from 160 px at 1080 px height, content from 40 px |
