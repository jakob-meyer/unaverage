# ORD Objective Order

**In one line:** A consistent column grid orders one sans in a few sizes, objective photography and exactly one signal color; trust comes from verifiable consistency, not from effects.

**Group** I Trust | **Lead effect** W1 Reassurance (also W2, W3, W4, W13; against W11, W12) | **Loudness** quiet (span 0 to 3 of 6) | **Material** none needed, objective photos help

## 1 Effect

Objective, precise, reliable, lasting, internationally legible. Decision makers read the design as considered and low-risk (H). Without a second decision it tips into cool and interchangeable: blue, a house typeface and a 16 px body size are shared by all enterprise references; they only become distinguishable through asceticism (IBM) or a form set (Siemens) (H).

## 3 Principle and evidence

Every edge, size and color has an ordering function; color is code with exactly one role.

- **School:** the journal Neue Grafik 1958 to 1965 [1], Müller-Brockmann (Grid Systems), Tschichold (asymmetry as function), Vignelli Canon (few typefaces, size jumps mostly by a factor of 2) [2], Aicher for Munich 1972 (color as code) (high).
- **Reference: IBM:** one typeface in three weights, corners 0 to 4 px, gray plus blue only for action, customer card with a number and a reference value (high for the system [3]).
- **Reference: Siemens:** dark brand frame, 0 px, a few defined forms, founder portrait in present-day colors; Red Dot 2024 Brand Identity [4] (high).
- **Reference: Carbon:** density as a parameter, layers instead of shadows (medium). **Reference: On:** category list at title size instead of icon tiles, color only from the campaign (medium). **Reference: Helsing:** only 0 px radii, no weight above 450, edge-to-edge caps line (medium, own measurement; design without an award). **Reference: Benedict Evans:** reading deck in a three-zone grid (medium).
- **Counterexamples:** Personio (eyebrow over 8 of 13 headings), Salesforce (14 sizes, mixed button shapes).

## 4 Parameters

**Typography**

| Parameter | Value |
|---|---|
| Classes | neo-grotesque or a sans with a width axis as the only family; mono only for values and specifications; serif only as a title voice in documents. Never adopt third-party house typefaces (IBM Plex, Siemens Sans) |
| Scale | Portal 2.2 to 4.5; Campaign live 2.0 to 3.5 with a photo as the large form; Poster with a caps line up to about 9; mobile from 2.0; slides 2 to 3.5 per slide; product UI at most 2.5 (consumer apps [README.md](README.md) 2.1) |
| Weights | 2 to 3 load-bearing roles, weight inverse to size: display over 50 px in 200 to 450, the hero headline in the Persuade register from 500 or in a display cut or an optical size ([craft.md](../craft.md) 5.1, point 2), small ordering words 600; heavy weights (700 to 900) only for short titles up to 50 px as an industrial code |
| Tracking | display 0 to -0.02 em (a text cut used at 64 px or more: down to -0.03 em); caps labels +0.05 to +0.1 em; body text 0 |
| Setting | flush left, ragged right; line 45 to 75 characters (target 66); line height text 1.45 to 1.6, display 1.0 to 1.1; chapter head in its own left column instead of a centered section head |

Pairings (starting points; license, embedding, figures and characters of the output language per pairing in [fonts.md](../fonts.md)):

- Pairing A: Archivo as the only family (display wdth 112 to 125 in 500, text wdth 100 in 400, tables wdth 87), Fragment Mono for values. Archivo is widespread and carries only through the width axis ([fonts.md](../fonts.md)).
- Pairing B: Albert Sans alone, display 300 (hero headline in the Persuade register 500), ordering words 600, text 400; no tabular figures, so rows of metrics in Fragment Mono ([fonts.md](../fonts.md)).
- Pairing C: Sofia Sans 400 and 600 with Sofia Sans Condensed for tables; in documents Source Serif 4 (opsz high, 500) as the title voice; public sector Public Sans ([fonts.md](../fonts.md)).

**Layout:** web 12 columns, mobile 4; slide 1920 x 1080 with 8 columns; base unit 8 px, spacing only 1x to 12x of it; text aligns to column edges. Density either Portal (dense and calm) or Poster (one object, one word per screen); chapters alternate between a dense zone (list, table, proof) and an open zone. White space active and asymmetric (an empty column instead of a margin all around); spacing within a group at most half the spacing between groups. Asymmetry is mandatory: hero and chapter heads flush left.

**Imagery:** objective photography (eye level or aerial, everything sharp, natural light, no grading [3]); real technology in use; one object in studio light with a person for scale; origin image in present-day colors; cutouts on a neutral ground.

**Form:** radii 0 to 4 px as exactly one family; 1 px hairlines instead of cards; no shadows; rectangular buttons; containers through layer gray.

**Signature elements** (at least one, never all): 1 visible column edges as hairlines, only where they order content; 2 a chapter-head column to the left of the content; 3 customer card as a fixed unit (logo, statement, number with unit and reference value); 4 oversized metric in the grid with a comparison; 5 category list at title size instead of icon tiles; 6 dark brand frame at the top and bottom, a light working surface in between; 7 a few defined forms from the brand's own logo geometry, each with a core idea; 8 an edge-to-edge caps line of the category as the loud dose.

**Motion:** hardly any; 150 to 250 ms ease-out; hard cuts and tab changes; grid cells fade in at most once in reading direction; no parallax, no counting-up numbers.

**Data:** gray plus signal, direct labeling, bars from zero, at most three light gridlines, tabular figures; every metric with unit and reference value (rules in [DAT.md](DAT.md)).

**Iconography:** a custom line pictogram system on a grid, stroke weight coupled to the type weight; icons only for navigation, status and wayfinding; no icon tiles.

## 6 Dose

| Level | What changes |
|---|---|
| quiet | white, signal only on the primary action, Portal density, scale about 3.3, grid invisible, reportage photos (reference: IBM) |
| medium | dark brand frame or hairlines on column edges, chapter-head column, one or two brand forms, one full-bleed photo per chapter (reference: Siemens, On) |
| loud | Poster: category word edge to edge with a cap height of about 10 to 15% of the screen height or a giant metric, one object per screen in studio light, no button in the first screen, light and dark with meaning (machine dark, human light; reference: Helsing) |

## 7 AI trap

- **AI proximity:** medium (a warning, not a ranking criterion).
- **Swiss pastiche:** red circle, black Helvetica or Inter, grid lines as ornament, rotated text without reading logic.
- **Broadsheet costume:** hairlines, radius 0, mono labels everywhere, section labels "01 /", decorative grid in the background.
- **Corporate mediocrity:** blue gradient, shield icon trio, stock photo, eyebrow over every chapter, centered section heads, three feature cards, stat banner.
- **Mechanism:** trait instead of principle; the category mean.
- **Review question:** Can every edge be traced back to a few columns, and does every size and every label have an ordering function?
- **Countermeasure:** fix the grid as numbers before the draft (columns, base unit, spacing ladder); name the second decision before building (signal with a role, image rule or brand form), otherwise you get category blue without a voice; choose the signal against the competition; plan composition changes (G13); mono only for specifications.
- **Watch list** ([generic-catalog.md](../generic-catalog.md)): tells A1, A4, A5, A6, B6, C1, C8, C11, D1, D3, D4, D5, E5; clusters Broadsheet and Stock hero; V3, V5, V8, V12, V23.

## 8 Media notes by register

| Register | What differs |
|---|---|
| Operate | the natural skin for team tools: density levels 24, 32, 40, 48 px with a coupled toolbar, selection as a mode change, layer gray instead of shadows, 0 px for functional corners (reference: Carbon) |
| Persuade | portal with chapter heads on the left; flush-left hero with one message and a real image; one button plus a text link; the trust signals the market expects, named concretely and few in number (DACH: supervisory authority or license and data location by name, at most four curated seals, mandatory notices at reading size; [markets/german.md](../markets/german.md) 7) |
| Read | reading deck in a three-zone grid (head about 20%, proof about 65%, footer zone about 15%, with a source in it only for third-party data; reference: Benedict Evans); assertion headline flush left at 56 to 64 px on 1080, at most two lines; one proof per slide; at most 7 layouts up to 40 slides. Factsheet in A4 or US Letter (by market) on 12 columns with a specification table in tabular figures and a margin column; print with bleed, Chrome PDF is RGB |
| Feed | stands out through calm and size, not color; photo with a frame and one number, or a portrait plus a date; header band and grid constant across all slides |
| Distance | Poster mode; cap height from the reading distance per the long-distance mode in [README.md](README.md); one message above head height |
