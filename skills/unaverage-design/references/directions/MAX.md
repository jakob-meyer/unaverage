# MAX Contained Maximalism

**In one line:** Color, pattern, shapes and a bold display typeface create joy, but locked into containers and with a hierarchy that can be found after one second.

**Group** V Expression | **Lead effect** W12 Joy (also W9, W11, W13; against W1, W5) | **Loudness** loud (span 4 to 6 of 6) | **Material** no outside material, but an ownable lead color and a shape set from the logo

How to read this, ground rules G1 to G14, registers and combination rules: [README.md](README.md).

## 1 Effect

- Cheerful, energetic, young, self-ironic, shareable. Audience: "This makes me want it, I'm sharing this." (H)
- Loud does not mean saturated: three of four loud B2C home pages are below 10% saturated pixels in the first screen; loudness comes from concentration on a lot of calm (medium).
- Risk: noise, fatigue, the wrong audience; the preference for colorfulness and complexity varies with age, education and country [1] (high).

## 3 Principle and evidence

Abundance in containers, order underneath: the frame stays calm, two to three axes get loud, exactly one element dominates per surface (medium).

- **School:** Memphis around Ettore Sottsass from 1981 [2]; Postmodernism, V&A 2011; "The New Ugly", Creative Review 2007 (high for the history).
- **References inside the corridor:** Reference: Spotify Wrapped (a fixed blueprint, one accent per card, a pattern set per year, the logo as play material; Cannes Lions 2023 Gold, high [3]). Reference: Duolingo (one signal color concentrated on 86% white, a louder sub-brand in its own dark section; Apple Design Award 2023, high [4]). Reference: Graza (lime and peach from the label, scattered verbs; D&AD 2023 Graphite Pencil, high [5]). Reference: Mailchimp, identity 2018 (yellow as a full-bleed surface; ADC 2019 Silver Cube, high; today's home page is no evidence). Reference: Deezer (purple over 40% of the area and still calm; medium). Reference: Koto deck (frame quiet, customer chapters loud; medium). Reference: Clay (chapter colors color ground, heading, button and image object together; medium).
- **Only as a principle, not in the measurement profile:** Reference: Material 3 Expressive (emphasis only on key elements; manufacturer study, medium).
- **Counterexample:** Slidesgo (decoration as the glue, 32% saturated without hierarchy).

## 4 Parameters

Two modes, set in the design contract, because the measurement profile is bimodal: **Color field** (the lead color as a large flat area; Mailchimp 2018, Deezer, Wrapped cards) and **Concentration** (abundance in containers on a lot of calm; Duolingo, Graza).

**Typography**

- Classes: a bold display with personality (rounded, narrow-industrial or wide) only for titles and numbers, or a variable display with a width axis for fit-to-width; a calm companion sans for everything else. Display never from L1 or L2 without a deliberate axis (G5): otherwise a scale from 5, line height up to 1.0 and weight from 700 produce the cluster Template display.
- Scale: web Concentration 3.5 to 8, Color field 6 to 10; social: the level-1 element 15 to 25% of the format height, secondary elements a factor of 3 to 4 smaller; deck up to about 9.
- Weights, tracking: display 700 to 900 (poster weight is right here), text 400; display -0.02 to -0.04 em, line height 0.9 to 1.0; the width follows the content.
- Setting: one statement per card; microcopy as two-liners with a dry punchline in the second line (Wrapped); naming from one word field, control labels unambiguous; the claim as an open sentence template; an accent word only with a reason from brand or content (tell A3).
- Pairing A: Anybody wdth 150 in 800 for display, wdth 50 for labels, Epilogue 400 for text ([fonts.md](../fonts.md), MAX).
- Pairing B: Unbounded 700 (wide) with Rethink Sans 400 ([fonts.md](../fonts.md), MAX).
- Pairing C: Dela Gothic One only in poster sizes with Work Sans 400 as the text voice ([fonts.md](../fonts.md), MAX).

**Color**

- Logic: own one lead color; three to five saturated tones plus black and white; additional colors only with a fixed meaning (genre, category, chapter), each as a scale; at most three hue ranges per surface; flat. Blocked: gradients, neon on near-black, gradient text; the violet-pink gradient is the AI default for "loud".
- Roles Color field: lead color 30 to 55%, black 15 to 25%, white 10 to 25%, patterns and additional colors at most 15%. Roles Concentration: white or neutral 70 to 85%, containers with abundance 15 to 30%.
- Saturation strategy (G4): Color field C (0.25 to 0.80), with a pastel lead color the lead color share counts instead of the saturation; Concentration B with a narrower value 0.04 to 0.20.
- Starter palette (placeholder; the brand color replaces the lead color, recompute contrasts):

**Layout:** order underneath: a grid following ORD as the frame; a fixed blueprint for series (zones, type and lockup stay; accent color, pattern and content change). Dense in the containers, calm around them, exactly one dominant element per surface. Every section a different composition from the same primitives; lock the loud parts into containers (tiles, emblems, packshots, a sub-brand section); frame quiet, content loud (Koto).

**Imagery:** the key surface shows a tangible image idea from the product's world and the offer or its use (a cutout on the color field, a photo in the container); lead color and pattern accompany it, they never replace it (SKILL.md stage 4, point 1). Plus patterns from logo building blocks, bold typography, shapes and stickers with meaning, collage, cut-out products, photos of real people and culture with a color flash or film grain, collaboration images, personal data as an image; stock not as imagery, only as a stand-in for ingredient, material, place until the brand's own photos arrive. Handwritten doodles only as M-HUM with an informal voice.

**Form:** a shape set of three to six basic shapes, derived from the logo; waves and zigzag only from the brand; one consistent radius decision (0 or soft); tactile buttons with depth through a colored bottom edge instead of a shadow (Duolingo); flat.

**Signature elements (at least one):**

1. Fit-to-width number or word, cropped (reference: Spotify Wrapped).
2. The lead color as a full-bleed surface across whole chapters (reference: Mailchimp 2018, Deezer).
3. A pattern set per campaign or year with a fixed number of patterns.
4. Scattered verbs or staircase setting as a one-time grid break (reference: Graza).
5. The logo as play material, the basic form untouched (reference: Spotify Wrapped).
6. Lock the louder part into its own stage (reference: Duolingo, sub-brand in a dark section).
7. Chapter colors that color ground, heading, button and image object together (reference: Clay).

**Motion:** springy and playful, but with one principle; beat and state changes in time; text beats 0.5 to 1.5 s; loops pausable with a visible switch (Graza: "Pause animations" in the footer); `prefers-reduced-motion`.

**Data:** personal data as an image (a cropped giant number); a ranking set typographically instead of progress bars; charts in the lead color without a rainbow; data honesty following DAT.

**Iconography:** stickers and emblems from the shape set instead of icons; no emoji as icons (tell C9).

## 6 Dose

| Level | What changes |
|---|---|
| quiet | mode Concentration: a neutral page, abundance only in containers (packshots, tiles, chapter openers), the lead color on the action (reference: Duolingo) |
| medium | the lead color as areas in every other chapter, patterns on title surfaces, shape set, fit-to-width typography (reference: Graza) |
| loud | mode Color field over 50%, patterns, animated logo states, additional colors with meaning, personal data as a giant number (reference: Spotify Wrapped, Mailchimp 2018, Deezer) |

## 7 AI trap

- **AI proximity:** high (a warning, not a ranking criterion).
- **Caricature:** confetti and squiggles spread evenly on pastel like wallpaper; "loud" as neon, glitch, gradient text, many colors and caps everywhere; rainbow gradient; a purple-pink glow with glass; an equalizer as decoration; blobs and plants as the glue; stickers everywhere; a dopamine palette without a reading path; Poppins or Montserrat in 800 as the poster voice.
- **Mechanism:** even distribution and overfulfillment.
- **Review question (also for R1 and R3, before the measurement findings):** What do you see first, what second, where is the container, and does every color have a job?
- **Countermeasure:** set the level-1 element before designing; separate the axes (two to three loud axes, the frame never loud); own a lead color instead of mixing a palette; color roles as a table; a shape set from the logo instead of from shape libraries; a contact sheet of the whole series before the polish; one repeated gesture instead of many effects.
- **Watch list:** tell A3, A8, B1, C1, C2, C3, C4, C9, D10; clusters Template display and Glass look; visual V2, V10, V13, V14 ([generic-catalog.md](../generic-catalog.md)).
