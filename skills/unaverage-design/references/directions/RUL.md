# RUL One Rule, Many Forms

**In one line:** One nameable rule, derived from logo, name or the audience's tools, generates many forms; the system with named states becomes the brand mark itself, even without a logo.

**Group** IV Idea and system | **Lead effect** W13 Recognition (also W1, W2, W14) | **Loudness** medium (span 2 to 5 of 6) | **Material** no outside material, but a primitive from name, logo or the audience's tool, and someone who maintains the system

How to read this, ground rules G1 to G14, registers and combination rules: [README.md](README.md).

## 1 Effect

- Conceptual, clever, consistent, extensible. Audience: "That's the brand again, even without the logo, and there is a thought behind it." (H)
- Long-lived: the stripes of the Dutch police (Studio Dumbar 1993) were adopted voluntarily by other emergency services [1] (high).
- Risk: a rule nobody sees; a system that decays without maintenance; in this skill, together with ORD, the most frequent direction in the set (each fixed in 5 of 9 trial cases, [README.md](README.md) 11.8).

## 3 Principle and evidence

An identity is a system of three anchors (sign, type, color) plus one variation rule. The rule comes from the subject and, with new content, generates a new, correct form (medium, studio cluster).

- **School:** Crouwel (Stedelijk, New Alphabet 1967), Studio Dumbar (PTT 1989, police 1993 [1]), Aicher (Munich 1972, color as code), Pentagram (MIT Media Lab 2014, grid from the logo [2]; Mastercard, parametric tools [3]), Experimental Jetset (Whitney 2013, only as a principle) (high).
- **References inside the corridor** (identities measured on work images, not on host pages): Reference: OpenAI DevDay (the developers' tool signs, one typeface; D&AD 2026 Yellow Pencil, high [4]). Reference: Faculty (threshold line from the claim, generator; D&AD 2025 Wood Pencil, medium [5]). Reference: Deezer (a heart of ellipses with named states; D&AD 2024 Shortlist, medium). Reference: Vevo (staggering and opacity; D&AD 2022 Wood Pencil Motion, medium [6]). Reference: Wise (two greens with fixed roles, a caps house typeface only for stance; ADC 2024 Merit, high [7]). Reference: Mews (notch from the logo; medium). Reference: Clay (chapter colors; medium). Reference: DeepL (color per product line; medium).
- **Only as a principle, not in the measurement profile:** Reference: Nothing (punctuation; six families), Reference: Siemens (shape set; corridors following ORD), Reference: Copilot Money (category pill in product UI).
- **Counterexample:** Personio (the same section formula eight times, only the background color changes).

## 4 Parameters

**Typography**

- Classes: one family with an expressive axis (width, mono, flare, italic) or a modular, code-like typeface; hierarchy over an unusual axis instead of over effects. Role model as an exception to G5: a loud house typeface only for stance sentences and numbers, at most 12% of the text, a neutral text typeface for everything explanatory (reference: Wise).
- Scale: website 3.5 to 7.5; social, system sheet and space following the primitive.
- Weights and tracking: 2 to 4 weights of one family; tracking fixed per width.
- Setting: hierarchy over exactly one axis: indent like code (DevDay), opacity 100, 75, 50, 25% (Vevo), width and weight (Deezer) or a change of voice; the lockup follows the rule; the claim as a sentence template with an exchangeable part.
- Pairing A: Geologica as the only family, axes CRSV, SHRP, slnt and wght are parameters of the rule ([fonts.md](../fonts.md), RUL).
- Pairing B: Martian Mono with width axis 75 to 112.5 as the variation parameter, Familjen Grotesk 400 for reading text ([fonts.md](../fonts.md), RUL).
- Pairing C (code-oriented audiences): Recursive as one family, axes MONO and CASL switch between sans and mono, seriousness and play ([fonts.md](../fonts.md), RUL).

**Color**

- Logic: color as code with meaning (syntax role, genre, product line, chapter, action versus seriousness); at most five code colors, each as a scale light, medium, dark. Free rainbow palettes age quickly (Robinhood 2024); violet only as a proven owned color (tell B1).
- Roles by mode: **Emphasis** neutrals 85 to 95%, code colors 3 to 10%, action under 3% (DevDay). **Image carrier** real photos within the system, also as duotone in system colors, code colors on frames and signs (Vevo). **Field** owned color 40 to 60%, black and white 30 to 50%, code colors at most 10% (Deezer).
- Saturation strategy (G4): Emphasis A or B up to 15%; Image carrier A or B on the surface, with photos 10 to 30% in total; Field C (30 to 65%).
- Starter palette (placeholder; the brand color replaces the owned color, recompute contrasts):

| Role | Hex | Contrast (WCAG) |
|---|---|---|
| White, black | #FFFFFF, #101010 | 19.03:1 |
| Light gray | #EDEDED | black on it 16.25:1 |
| Owned color signal orange | #FF5A1F | black on it 6.10:1; white on it only 3.12:1, so large type only |
| Code petrol | #0F7B6C | 5.16:1 on white; on light gray 4.41:1, large type only there |
| Code magenta | #B3175A | 6.59:1 on white; 5.63:1 on light gray |
| Code mustard | #8A6A00 | 5.07:1 on white; on light gray 4.33:1, large type only there |

Create every code color in three steps (light area, mid tone, dark text) and recompute per step.

**Layout:** the primitive determines the grid (7 x 7 at MIT Media Lab, staggering at Vevo; the bracket frames, the notch cuts). Medium density, a fixed stage with a changing variable; pauses as an empty surface with a single sign (DevDay); logo small, system large (Vevo); a change of composition is mandatory (G13); a system sheet with at least six touchpoints.

**Imagery:** an object or a situation from the product's world carries the key surface, and the primitive frames, marks or counts it; the primitive alone is never the main image (SKILL.md stage 4, point 1). Plus real content (artists, event photos, employees, customer material) within the system; a symbolic object from the product core with material texture (Wise: a painted globe with coins). No stock images as imagery; as stand-ins for real content only until delivery.

**Form:** derived from the primitive (notch, ellipse, bracket, threshold); radius and line weight follow the primitive; hairlines systematic; shadows only when the rule defines depth.

**Signature elements (at least one):**

1. Logo as a kit with three to six named states (reference: Deezer).
2. Parametric generator with SVG export for rule-conforming variance (reference: Vevo, Faculty).
3. The audience's tool sign as the primitive (reference: OpenAI DevDay).
4. Primitive taken literally from the claim (reference: Faculty, threshold).
5. Color as a chapter or product-line system on the same stage (reference: Clay, DeepL).
6. Opacity steps as hierarchy (reference: Vevo).
7. The promise as a diagram in brand dress (reference: Wise, bar length equals the amount received).
8. Punctuation as the brand mark (reference: Nothing, brackets around model numbers).

**Motion:** the rule in motion (scale, stagger, swing, wipe) across three to six named states; motion identity 300 to 600 ms, UI transitions shorter ([modules.md](modules.md), M-MOT); moving image goes to a video skill.

**Data:** data visualization in brand dress (Vevo step bars, Mews columns, Wise bars), with data honesty following DAT.

**Iconography:** a numbered icon set derived from the primitives, or pictograms on a grid (Aicher principle); no standard set.

## 5 Measurable targets

Modes, set in the design contract: color **Emphasis**, **Image carrier** or **Field**; composition **System surface** (logo, card, poster, system sheet) or **Image stage** (collage, cover grid, chapters with photos). Measure identities on work images, websites on the first screen at 1440 x 900. Corridors are starting values (H) and include all references inside the corridor from section 3; tolerance in [review.md](../review.md).

## 7 AI trap

- **AI proximity:** medium (a warning, not a ranking criterion).
- **Caricature:** diagonal stripes, Helvetica and primary colors without a rule; decorative grid lines; random candy colors; "logo variants" without a system; code snippets as texture; the logo large in every hero; the same section formula with a changing background color; rebuilding the signatures of living studios (G8).
- **Mechanism:** trait instead of principle; the rule is missing, only its traces are there.
- **The skill's own trap:** Because RUL needs no material, it often wins. Across many brands "a primitive with named states" otherwise becomes the skill's house style. Countermeasure: rotation through the direction log (SKILL.md, stage 7), ask about material before the ranking.
- **Review question (also for R1 and R3, before the measurement findings):** Can the rule be named in one sentence, does it generate, with new content, a new, correct form across at least six touchpoints, and is the brand recognizable with the logo covered?
- **Countermeasure:** derive the primitive from three sources (signs of the audience, the image in the positioning statement, building blocks of the logo; [exploration.md](../exploration.md)); record the rule in writing with its states; build a small generator in HTML and SVG; a contact sheet of all formats before the polish.
- **Watch list:** tell A4, A5, A6, B1, C11, D5, F1; visual V13, V19, V23, V25 ([generic-catalog.md](../generic-catalog.md)).

## 8 Media notes by register

| Register | What differs |
|---|---|
| Operate | data categories and states as the primitive (a color pill with a sign; reference: Copilot Money), the primitive in empty and loading states; controls neutral following the Operate register; in the design system (Case C9) as tokens with state names |
| Persuade | chapters per product line with their own color and their own primitive state; logo small; the primitive in navigation and transitions; a change of composition in addition to the color change. System sheet (6 to 12 touchpoints) only for identity (Cases D1, D2, X3); otherwise a mockup as the key surface following [exploration.md](../exploration.md) 7.2, states of the primitive in the system strip |
| Read | the primitive as chapter mark, chapter color per section, chart style in brand dress; brand board and report with chapter colors |
| Feed | series kit: constant (primitive, template, header band) and variable (content, chapter color) in the contract; a contact sheet of all formats; in the platform-native feed mode only subtitles and end card |
| Distance | wayfinding, trade fair, stage: the primitive as a spatial element (DevDay: bracket signage); one state per surface, test at 10% size |
