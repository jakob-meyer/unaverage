# WRM Warm Closeness

**In one line:** Real people in concrete everyday scenes, daylight, soft forms and tinted neutrals make competence approachable and take away the fear of a decision; depth comes from photo and surface, never from effects.

**Group** III Closeness | **Lead effect** W8 Closeness (also W1, W6, W9, W10, W12) | **Loudness** quiet to medium (span 1 to 3 of 6) | **Material** photos of real people with consent; without them downgraded, request a shot list, no generated people

How to read this, ground rules G1 to G14, registers and combination rules: [README.md](README.md).

## 1 Effect

- Approachable, reassuring, trustworthy without strictness, close to everyday life. Audience: "They know what my everyday life looks like, and they make it easy for me." (H)
- Real people get looked at, stock photos get ignored (medium [1]). Everyday settings brought +32.8% emotion in B2B tech spots; personal benefit works about twice as strongly as business benefit, even in B2B (both medium). The threshold drops for money and health.
- Risk: interchangeability. The D2C kit looks just as friendly (counterexample: Allbirds, generic index 0 and still visibly a kit).

## 3 Principle and evidence

- **Mechanism of effect:** people in relationship at a concrete place, in action instead of pose, work more strongly than cut-out products (medium [1]).
- **Form from nature and emphasis:** motion from wave, growth, pulse, orbit (Natural History Museum, Pentagram with Nomad 2023 [2]); emphasis instead of ornament, key elements found up to four times faster (Material 3 Expressive, manufacturer study with 46 studies, medium [3]). Form trends such as "Soft Edges" come only from trend reports and are no evidence.
- **References (inside the corridor):** Reference: N26 (one offer per screen in snapping chapters, UI fragments over everyday photos, 0 gradients, 0 glows, 0 shadows; effect high, design without an award). Reference: Mercury (banking as a calm place, day-night change of the ground, tactile objects instead of icons; medium). Reference: Gentler Streak (corridor instead of a finish line, user status as a system state, the person gets the brand color; Apple Design Award 2024 for the app, high [4]). Reference: Material 3 Expressive (form as a carrier of meaning, skin of personal apps).
- **Counterexample:** Allbirds (natural tones not tied to material or product).

## 4 Parameters

**Typography**

- Classes: humanist or friendly sans; optionally a warm contrast serif for encouraging headlines; strength through width instead of weight (an extended width for headlines, reference: N26).
- Scale: desktop 4 to 6, mobile 2.1 to 3.3; app following the Operate register (at most 2.5 per screen; consumer apps [README.md](README.md) 2.1).
- Weights: display 400 to 500, at most 700, never Black; sublines with variable fonts 360 to 400 (reference: Mercury); at most three visible levels.
- Tracking: display 0 or slightly positive; body text +0.01 to +0.02 em.
- Setting: set the form of address and keep it (address per market; German: [markets/german.md](../markets/german.md) 2); one promise per chapter; encouragement instead of pressure, encouragement as its own headline level; mandatory notices in their own section at reading size (from 16 px); customer and patient texts from 18 px.
- Pairing A: Gloock 400 for titles, Figtree 400 and 500 for text ([fonts.md](../fonts.md), WRM).
- Pairing B: Literata (opsz 72, 500) for titles, Golos Text 400 for text ([fonts.md](../fonts.md), WRM).
- Pairing C (health, legibility): Petrona 500 for titles, Atkinson Hyperlegible Next 400; width variant Archivo wdth 125 ([fonts.md](../fonts.md), WRM).

**Color**

- Logic: tinted neutrals (OKLCH chroma 0.005 to 0.02) plus one deep brand color as a panel; carry the brand color into the photography (clothing, props); light and dark with meaning (day and night); the person gets the brand color, data gets neutral or status colors. Green is close to the category in European fintech: choose another deep tone there.
- Roles: photos 40 to 70%; light areas 20 to 35%; deep-tone panels 10 to 25%; action under 3%; warm area at most 10%.
- Saturation strategy B with a narrower value: 2 to 22%, color from photos and one brand area.
- Starter palette (placeholder; the brand color replaces panel and action, recompute contrasts):

**Layout:** 12 columns, a narrow type area (about 70% of the width); density low to medium, one offer per screen, optionally snapping chapters; a light-dark change structures without dividers; panels with a large radius over a real-life photo instead of a card grid; the hero may be centered, content left-aligned; UI fragments as chips in the photo.

**Imagery:** real people in relationship at concrete places, in action instead of pose; everyday life instead of the office; daylight; no frontal gaze into the camera as the default; a familiar object in a calm, unexpected place (reference: Mercury). Consent of the people shown ([compliance.md](../compliance.md) 3; EU and DACH: [markets/eu-dach-compliance.md](../markets/eu-dach-compliance.md) 9); no generated people; stock only following an image brief, as a stand-in for place or situation, depending on the claim of the surface, until the brand's own photos arrive (faces only as mood, never as customers or team); until then the direction stays downgraded. Instead of icons, tactile single objects from one material family. Cheapest real route: team or customer photos with a phone following the guide ([images-and-assets.md](../images-and-assets.md)), about one hour plus consents; substitute: people without a recognizable face (hands, backs, workplace) from the brand's own photos.

**Form:** radii as one family from the product (card, packaging, app icon), 12 to 48 px; pill buttons; hardly any lines, structure through light-dark and spacing; flat: no glows, gradients only as image darkening, shadows at most occasionally; softness from grain and blur in the image instead of from effects; containers as a panel over a photo, not as a tile.

**Signature elements (at least one):**

1. UI fragment chip (one value, one line) over an everyday photo (reference: N26).
2. Brand color in the photo (clothing, props).
3. Day-night dramaturgy of the page ground (reference: Mercury).
4. Corridor or tolerance band instead of a finish line (reference: Gentler Streak).
5. Tactile object family from one material family instead of icons.
6. Encouraging tone as its own headline level.
7. Trust block: license and deposit insurance in plain words, at most four curated seals.

**Motion:** soft but short: UI 200 to 350 ms ease-out, scene changes 300 to 500 ms, snapping chapters; springs only at confirmation and success; one motion idea from nature or name (wave, growth, pulse); reduced motion as a complete version.

**Data:** numbers on a human scale (amount, time, distance); corridor instead of target; comparison with context (direction symbol plus previous period in plain words, a rating word) instead of red and green badges; a chart form of its own per metric.

**Iconography:** a rounded line system, stroke matching the type weight, fill for the active state; no emoji icons, no circle icon row "secure, fast, simple", no flat figures from free sets.

## 7 AI trap

- **AI proximity:** high (a warning, not a ranking criterion).
- **Caricature:** beige blobs, monstera leaf, pastel, terracotta gradient, smiling stock people looking frontally into the camera, hands holding a heart, umbrella and hands for insurance, a purple-pink gradient with a floating 3D credit card, an icon trio of shield, heart and leaf, flat figures with long limbs.
- **D2C kit (visual check only, the tool does not detect it):** yes or no per point: floating navigation with a radius? rounded hero with an inset? colored category tiles with a pill label? pill outline on photos? three text cards with a mono eyebrow? sparkle button? Two yes answers mean a change of course (H).
- **Mechanism:** "warm" as a color filter over everything; form without origin; missing material.
- **Review question (also for R1 and R3, before the measurement findings):** Are these real people at a concrete place in an action, and does every soft form come from product, nature or name?
- **Countermeasure:** image brief with casting, location, light and prohibitions; without photos downgrade and request a shot list; one radius family from the product; panels instead of tiles; warmth from light and material instead of from pastel areas.
- **Watch list:** tell B1, B3, C3, C6, C8, C9, C10, D1, D3; cluster Card kit; visual V8, V9, V11, V14 ([generic-catalog.md](../generic-catalog.md)).

## 8 Media notes by register

| Register | What differs |
|---|---|
| Operate | skin of personal apps: emphasis only on the main action and the current state; user status as a system state ("Pause"); a personal color for the person, neutral for data; radii 12 to 24 px; primary action 44 px |
| Persuade | app download page with snapping chapters, one button per chapter, trust block in plain words; on mobile its own portrait motif |
| Read | customer and patient information with 18 px body text, short sections, photos, corridor charts; in the deck the customer situation before the product, image masks with a large radius, chapters as a deep-tone area |
| Feed | everyday scene 4:5 with one sentence or one UI fragment; a series through recurring people or places; Reels and paid social B2C in the platform-native feed mode |
| Distance | one person, one sentence, brand color as a panel; face and claim above head height |
