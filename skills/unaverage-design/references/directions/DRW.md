# DRW Drawn World

**In one line:** A character or illustration world of its own with a fixed technique, theme and function carries feeling, explanation and recognition; grid, reading text and operation stay calm and conventional.

**Group** III Closeness | **Lead effect** W9 Likability (also W3, W8, W12, W13; against W5) | **Loudness** medium to loud (span 2 to 5 of 6) | **Material** commissioned illustration or a very simple SVG technique that an agent draws consistently; without it downgraded

How to read this, ground rules G1 to G14, registers and combination rules: [README.md](README.md).

## 1 Effect

- Likable, distinctive, explanatory, warm, memorable. Audience: "I like them, and I'd recognize them instantly, anywhere." (H)
- Campaigns with a brand character show very large profit effects more often (34.1% vs. 26.2% in IPA long-term campaigns, medium). The more intangible the product, the more tangible the branding must be (medium). Explanatory images in the Holmes style are read just as well and remembered better after two to three weeks (small study, medium).
- Risk: a character as a sticker without a role, flat style from free sets; a recurring element only works through years of repetition.

## 3 Principle and evidence

- **Origin:** Isotype and Nigel Holmes as an explanatory visual language; the studio practice of Collins (Mailchimp 2018 with several illustrators [4]); johnson banks with Fontsmith for Duolingo 2019, a display of its own as a deliberate turn away from neutral sans (high [3]).
- **References, mode White:** Reference: Duolingo (character with an ensemble, one saturated signal color on about 86% white, tactile forms; Apple Design Award 2023, high [2]). Reference: Notion (unfilled line figures look over window edges, a book serif as the human voice; medium).
- **References, mode Color world:** Reference: Clay (a clay marble run as a physical metaphor through the whole page, chapter colors, illustrator in the footer; medium). Reference: Robinhood 2020 (a utopian future world explains financial terms; D&AD Yellow and Graphite Pencil, high [1]). Reference: Mailchimp 2018 (yellow as a full-bleed surface, surreal hand drawing; ADC 2019). Robinhood and Mailchimp 2018 are measured on case study images; type and DOM values of their host pages do not count.
- **Counterexamples:** the Mailchimp home page today (imagery removed, standard section sequence 5 of 5); Salesforce (character as a sticker).

## 4 Parameters

**Typography**

- Classes: a calm sans as the working voice; a character typeface in small doses; a serif as the human voice for quotes and stance. Strongest option: derive a display from the character's form language (reference: Duolingo).
- Scale: desktop 3.5 to 7.5; the hero may be quiet when the image is loud.
- Weights: rounded display 500 to 700, text 400.
- Tracking: display -0.02 to -0.05 em, text 0.
- Setting: short, countable promises instead of feature lists; wit from the audience's professional vocabulary in calm sentences, without emoji and exclamation marks.
- Pairing A: Tilt Warp (rotation axes, only titles and numbers) with Onest 400 and 500 ([fonts.md](../fonts.md), DRW).
- Pairing B: Onest 400 as the working voice, Fanwood Text with Italic as the human voice ([fonts.md](../fonts.md), DRW).
- Pairing C: Golos Text 400 as the working voice, Bagel Fat One only for large single numbers and reward moments ([fonts.md](../fonts.md), DRW).

**Color**

- Logic: the action color is separate from the personality; the illustration palette with three to six fixed tones carries the personality. Mode White: line on white with one concentrated lead color. Mode Color world: lead color or illustration as areas. Chapter colors only when they color ground, heading, button and image object of a chapter together (reference: Clay).
- Roles mode White: white 75 to 85%; line and text 10 to 20%; lead color 3 to 10%; action under 3%. Mode Color world: lead color or illustration 40 to 60%, a calm stage around the imagery, action under 3%.
- Saturation strategy: mode White A or B up to 10%; mode Color world C (35 to 80%).
- Starter palette mode White (placeholder; derive the lead color from character or name, recompute contrasts):

| Role | Hex | Contrast (WCAG) |
|---|---|---|
| Ground | #FFFFFF | |
| Line and text | #1A1A1A | 17.40:1 |
| Lead color poppy as area | #FF5B36 | text #1A1A1A on it 5.64:1; white on it 3.09:1, only from 24 px or 18.5 px bold |
| Lead color as text | #D9381C | 4.63:1 |
| Illustration tones | #FFD23F, #3FA7D6, #2E8B57 | only in illustrations; yellow on white 1.44:1, never as text |

**Layout:** a conventional grid; every section a different composition from the same primitives; density medium; mode White very calm, mode Color world image-filling. The character interacts with the product (looks over the window edge, leans on edges, never just stands next to it); one metaphor runs through the entry, chapters, convergence and close; no icon tiles.

**Imagery:** an imagery brief in writing before the first image: technique (unfilled line, ink, clay, thick flat objects, isometric drawing, collage), theme (an invented world or an ensemble), color, function (show, explain, react), proportions, states; one illustrator or a guide that unites several hands; name the author. Without an illustrator the agent draws following the SVG technique in [images-and-assets.md](../images-and-assets.md): one grid, one stroke width in display pixels, round caps, at most one fill color on under 25% of the figure, one perspective, concrete objects from the project world instead of figures with emotional expression, displayed from 96 px; render every figure once and look at it at 100%. A character with a role needs an illustrator, otherwise choose another direction. Generated illustration only after approval, as a uniform series with a measurement gate; never imitate the style of living illustrators. Free flat sets (unDraw, Humaaans, Open Peeps) are generic.

**Form:** derived from the character: tactile buttons with a bottom edge instead of a shadow, thick objects with a side face (reference: Duolingo); one radius decision (button about 12 px); lines only in the stroke width of the imagery (H); no glows, no glass; containers stay calm, the imagery carries.

**Signature elements (at least one):**

1. Main character with an ensemble and named states (happy, waiting, sleeping, error).
2. The character reaches into the product (reference: Notion).
3. One physical metaphor for an abstract product, carried through all chapters (reference: Clay).
4. An illustration explains a technical term as an image (reference: Robinhood).
5. Reward objects as visual vocabulary (reference: Duolingo).
6. Photos in a duotone of the system colors connect photo and drawing (reference: Notion).
7. A dedicated icon set from the imagery instead of standard icons.

**Motion:** the character behaves: short loops of 1 to 2 s, tied to events, no perpetual loop; character reaction as UI feedback 200 to 400 ms; springs at success moments; pausable; reduced motion as a complete version.

**Data:** metaphors explain terms, numbers stand soberly next to them (Holmes principle); progress as a form or reward object instead of a percentage; Isotype for countable quantities.

**Iconography:** a dedicated set from the imagery for marketing; neutral functional icons in the UI; no icon tiles, no standard sets as visual language.

| Level | What changes |
|---|---|
| quiet | mode White, line figures only at the edges of the product surface, in empty states and chapter openers (reference: Notion) |
| medium | a character with an ensemble as the anchor of every section, one concentrated signal color, tactile forms (reference: Duolingo) |

## 7 AI trap

- **AI proximity:** high (a warning, not a ranking criterion).
- **Caricature:** Corporate Memphis figures with long limbs and small heads from free sets; glossy 3D clay icons; generated clay with exactly the smoothness that a craft brand is built against; a mascot as a sticker in tiles; emoji as personality.
- **Mechanism:** missing material and even distribution; the character gets no job.
- **Review question (also for R1 and R3, before the measurement findings):** Does the character have a role (shows, explains, reacts), and could it belong only to this brand?
- **Countermeasure:** derive the character from name or subject; an imagery brief before the first image (technique, proportions, states); commission an illustrator or choose a simple technique an agent can handle; image generation only with approval and a measurement gate.
- **Watch list:** tell C8, C9, C10, C12, D3, D9; cluster Card kit; visual V14, V20 ([generic-catalog.md](../generic-catalog.md)). Tell C12 inevitably fires for agent-drawn figures: allowed only with an imagery brief, a fixed technique and a role for the character, reported as intentional in the wrap-up.

## 8 Media notes by register

| Register | What differs |
|---|---|
| Operate | the character only in empty states, short onboarding, success, error and as a reward object; working surfaces free of illustration |
| Persuade | characters interact with product excerpts; the metaphor leads through the page; author credit in the footer; on mobile keep the character, rebuild the compositions; app store screenshots with real UI, the character only as a frame |
| Read | an explanatory image per chapter in the report; the character on title and chapter slides; data slides sober following DAT |
| Feed | a series with a recurring character or situation; the character's tone constant across all posts; one thought per image; story with a character reaction |
| Distance | the character large with exactly one sentence; merch in few solid colors (H) |
