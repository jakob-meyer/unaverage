# EDI Editorial Story

**In one line:** The brand's own photography, shown large, and a characterful display cut tell the story in an alternation of loud and quiet pages; text and image stand side by side or one after the other, never layered over each other as a veil.

**Group** II Value and origin | **Lead effect** W6 Relevance (also W3, W5, W8, W10, W15) | **Loudness** medium (span 2 to 4 of 6) | **Material** the brand's own photography, client material or a public-domain archive; without it downgraded, request an image brief and a shot list; until then free photos of place, material, situation or problem as stand-ins, depending on the claim of the surface ([images-and-assets.md](../images-and-assets.md) 5)

## 1 Effect

Curated, narrative, high-quality, confident, human. It is read like a magazine; the brand acts as an author with a stance (H). Real people and product details get looked at, stock photos get skipped [5] (medium); people interacting with each other at a concrete place work more strongly than text on screen (medium). Without strong photography only the serif costume remains.

## 3 Principle and evidence

A rhythm of loud and quiet: a large real image and a display cut with character alternate with calm reading zones; the caption is the trust layer.

- **School:** Alexey Brodovitch, art director of Harper's Bazaar from 1934: the spread as a stage of large photography, Bodoni and white space [1]; the zine and magazine tradition (high).
- **Reference: Here Design:** a photo book in slides, titles with the name upright and the idea in italics, six type sizes in 38 slides; a studio with 12 D&AD awards [2] (high).
- **Reference: Lando Norris:** the helmet livery as a system, a serif and sans mix in the setting, captions with place and year; Awwwards Site of the Year 2025 [3] (high). **Reference: Scout Motors:** chapter words at 270 px, archive against present, closing band; Awwwards E-Commerce of the Year 2025 [4] (high).
- **Reference: On:** a sports brand as a fashion magazine with a checkout. **Reference: Helsing:** one object per screen, a person for scale, light and dark with meaning. **Reference: Glossier:** the wordmark as a large form. **Reference: Stripe Press:** quiet reading mode (each medium).
- **Reference: Mailchimp 2018** (R/GA with Collins, ADC 2019 Silver Cube Art Direction [6]; medium) only as an archived state: today's home page with a card grid and a standard section sequence is a counterexample.

## 4 Parameters

**Typography**

| Parameter | Value |
|---|---|
| Classes | display: a high-contrast serif (Didone, transitional, narrow display serif) or a characterful display sans; text: a text serif or a calm sans; italic as the second voice; three levels: display, running text, caption; caps or mono only for captions with data |
| Scale | a large form is mandatory: live display from scale 4, or a flat live scale with a photo, video or wordmark as the large form. Stage 4 to 20; Big image live 1.4 to 3.5; Quiet 1.5 to 2.5; Deck 3 to 4 |
| Weights | serif display regular or light, serif never bold; heavy sans display (700) only with a line height of 0.83 to 1.1 |
| Tracking | serif display 0 to -0.02 em, sans display down to -0.03 em; caps captions +0.06 to +0.12 em at 11 to 13 px (under 12 px only when it is not required reading, and with 4.5:1); reading text 0 |
| Setting | titles on two lines, the subject upright, the idea in italics, as a whole line, never as a single accent word in a sans headline (tell A3); caption with place and year in small letterspaced caps; reading text 17 to 21 px, 60 to 70 characters; option: two-voice setting with a fixed rule, serif for the emotional words, sans for facts, at most one to three serif words per sentence; at most one pull quote per chapter |

Pairings (starting points; check status per pairing in [fonts.md](../fonts.md)):

- Pairing A: Imbue (opsz 100, 300 to 500) for titles, Piazzolla 400 for text, Commit Mono for captions and sources ([fonts.md](../fonts.md)).
- Pairing B: Noto Serif Display (wdth 62.5 to 100, 300 to 400, with italic) for titles, Libre Franklin 400 for text; no real minus sign, so negative numbers in Libre Franklin ([fonts.md](../fonts.md)).
- Pairing C (sans display for sport, automotive, deep tech): Schibsted Grotesk 700 over Literata 400 in the text, newspaper logic with a slight brand echo ([fonts.md](../fonts.md)).

Playfair Display, Cormorant, Fraunces, Instrument Serif, Newsreader, DM Serif, Lora and Young Serif are the typefaces of the magazine costume: as a display voice only with exactly one deliberate axis and a reason, otherwise yellow (tell A1).

**Color**

| Parameter | Value |
|---|---|
| Logic | surface almost colorless, color from the photography; areas only paper, black and one natural tone; one ink from the image for chapter numbers and emphasis; third-party photos and archive images as a duotone in the page tone |
| Saturation strategy (G4) | A or B tight: surface without a photo up to 5%, color otherwise from photos; with photos in the first screen at most 20% saturated pixels; ink under 3% of the text characters |
| Shares | photography 50 to 80%; ground 20 to 40%; text 5 to 10%; dark chapter optional 10 to 30% |

**Layout:** 12 columns with asymmetric splits; the stage is the screen width or the spread. Rhythm of loud and quiet: full bleed, text zone, image sequence; text bundled in its own zones or slides (reference: Here Design, 5 of 38 slides with text). Text keeps 112 to 135 px distance from the full-bleed image. Images full-bleed and cropped (a detail instead of a whole packshot); exactly one grid break per page.

**Imagery:** image brief before building ([images-and-assets.md](../images-and-assets.md)): two to three categories (portrait, reportage, detail), perspective, light, sharpness, editing, casting, format, text zone, bans (handshake, light bulb, team at a laptop); real people at real places with consent; archive, old against new; public-domain museum archives as a distinctive source.

**Form:** radius 0; hairlines under chapter heads; no cards, no shadows; the image to the edge; a frame as passe-partout; optionally one repeated form primitive from the brand object.

**Signature elements** (at least one): 1 title with the subject upright and the idea in italics; 2 a caption system with place, year and author in letterspaced caps (in the talk deck authors collected, [images-and-assets.md](../images-and-assets.md) 12); 3 an oversized chapter word in the image like a place-name sign; 4 the wordmark as a large form across the full width; 5 light and dark change of the ground by chapter meaning; 6 the footer as a closing image with claim and main motif; 7 two-voice setting with a fixed rule.

**Motion:** cinematic and slow: image changes 400 to 800 ms as a crossfade or wipe; image before text; one orchestrated moment per page; video with a visible pause button; exactly one motion idea from the content.

**Data:** a few large numbers set editorially (serif figures, in the text or as a pull quote); charts per [DAT.md](DAT.md) with a serif title and the ink as the accent.

**Iconography:** almost none; navigation and arrows typographic.

## 6 Dose

| Level | What changes |
|---|---|
| quiet | a reading sequence with one serif on paper, one image per chapter as a passe-partout, scale 1.5 to 3 (reference: Stripe Press) |
| medium | full-bleed photos, image and text alternating per chapter, italic as the second voice, caption system, dark chapters (reference: Here Design, On) |
| loud | chapter words over 200 px in the photo, wordmark across the full width, two-voice setting, video hero, footer as a closing scene (reference: Lando Norris, Scout Motors) |

## 7 AI trap

- **AI proximity:** high, the second wave of AI genericness (warning).
- **Caricature "magazine costume":** cream ground, Playfair or Instrument Serif with an italic accent word, letterspaced caps labels with hairlines, "Issue No. 01", invented page numbers, a stock photo with a black gradient and a headline over it; a broadsheet with mono labels and no image.
- **Mechanism:** missing material, and trait instead of principle.
- **Review question:** Does at least one real image carry the surface, and does the rhythm change across the sequence?
- **Countermeasure:** image brief before the layout; without photography downgrade or request photos specifically; text never over the main photo but in a band, margin or field; italics only when the idea can be named; issue and page numbers only with a real sequence.
- **Watch list** ([generic-catalog.md](../generic-catalog.md)): tells A1, A3, A4, A6, B3, C1, D7; clusters Cream look, Broadsheet, Template display; V8, V11, V14.

## 8 Media notes by register

| Register | What differs |
|---|---|
| Operate | only editorial areas (magazine, help, profiles, story onboarding); operating parts stay in the Operate register |
| Persuade | longform; hero with one image, one sentence, no button or one; chapters change the composition; captions as the trust layer; on mobile a motif of its own instead of a crop (reference: On) |
| Read | creds, story and founder deck: per chapter a title image with a two-line title, one text slide in a half-and-half scheme, then a text-free image sequence; 5 to 7 slide types; reading deck body text from 22 px, metadata from 18 px (reference: Here Design). Report with photo sequences, annual report, employer brochure, magazine with spreads and a margin column; newsletter as HTML text with one image |
| Feed | photo 4:5 full-bleed, text in a band in the safe area; the caption as the second reading level; carousel as a reportage with one text slide |
| Distance | talk version with one image and one chapter word per slide, content from 40 px at 1080; poster with a chapter word in the photo like a place-name sign |
