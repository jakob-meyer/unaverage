# Startup

As of 2026-10-10. Profiles for cases A1, A3, B2, B4, C7, D3, J2, J3. Matrix, gates (minimum type size, sizes) and render paths in [README.md](README.md), ranking in [directions/README.md](../directions/README.md). Evidence strength high, medium, H (derivation); sources [n] at the end.

## Group rules

- **Material is often missing** (no finished product, no photos, no customers): the material gate hits PRO, WRM, EDI and STI most often here. Best-form stand-in plus asset request instead of a stock team or invented logos (ground rule G7; [images-and-assets.md](../images-and-assets.md) 5). The stand-in shows the claim of the surface, not just the topic: software as a mockup from the features in the brief, a physical product as an illustration from its description, free photos of the problem, situation or material. If none shows the claim, the motif gets a final-form slot instead of a topic photo. Gray areas and placeholder titles look unfinished and also lower credibility.
- **Show instead of describe:** one tangible idea from the product's world carries every work, and one concrete case runs as a through-line across all surfaces. A mockup from the features in the brief with consistent sample data from that case is design, not fake UI ([SKILL.md](../../SKILL.md), Limits). Fake UI invents features, customers or key figures, or poses as a real screenshot.
- **Clarity before design:** if the purpose sentence or the core thesis is missing, report that back instead of setting it beautifully (medium [1][2]).
- **One asset system for everything:** pitch, website, launch and press kit share the same one or two own assets (ground rule G3).
- **One source, many versions:** reading and talk version, desktop and mobile, all social formats from one HTML source with variables.
- **Data rules** (decks, updates, website): assertion titles, gray plus one accent, direct labels, unit, bars from zero, no dual axis, no cumulative curve as growth, percent with an absolute base, source and date for third-party figures, collected in a footnote or the appendix; the company's own statements without a note ([DAT.md](../directions/DAT.md); [SKILL.md](../../SKILL.md) stage 4, point 4).

## Case A1 Pitch deck

Top 12, rank 11. Who: S very high, every round, often two versions. Register: Read (sent) and Distance (presented). Evidence: Reference: Pitch Series B, Reference: Benedict Evans (medium); counterexample Reference: Gamma.

- **Specs:** 1920 x 1080. Structure following Sequoia: purpose in one sentence, problem, solution, why now, market, competition, business model, team, financials, vision (medium [1]). Thesis on the first and the last slide, address risks yourself, appendix for expected objections (medium [3]). Chapter slides from about 12 slides; 3 to 7 slide types; at most 4 points per list, 2 lines per point (high [4]). Story slides (why now, competition, vision) are written as drafts from brief and category and marked in the wrap-up, never as placeholder titles; open secondary details (source, period, secondary figure) are dropped in the presentation view when the slide works without them, or phrased with what is known; the company's own statements stand without a note, sources only for third-party figures, collected in a footnote or the appendix, depiction notes only in the review view; everything open is in the review view. **Decision-critical slides are never missing:** team with track record, competition, use of funds and milestones (the ask slide says what the money is for). Ask about them in the kickoff, otherwise derive them from the brief; the slide works with what is known, open items stand in final form on their working slide ([SKILL.md](../../SKILL.md) stage 4, point 6: phrased with what is known, a card, row or field only with its value, never a label without one, a "to be added" label or dashed box), none on stage slides (title, solution, strongest figure, ask), at most one per slide and three in the deck, everything else only in the review view and the wrap-up; if the use of funds is open, the ask shows the known parts as figures, and the open split goes into the wrap-up as a question instead of a row without a value; never leave it out and never evade ("details in the meeting"). The problem slide shows the problem in the image, not the topic. The solution slide shows the offer: software as a mockup from the features in the brief, through which the through-line case runs; a physical product as a photo, cutout or illustration in the situation it is made for. Rhythm following [craft.md](../craft.md) 5.5: stage slides for title, solution, strongest figure and ask alternate with working slides; even in calm directions at least two full-bleed surfaces (color from the brand world or an image bleeding off the edge) and one color ground; an image at most twice, a signature element or a component at most three times in full form, otherwise as a crop or detail.
- **Minimum type size:** reading deck, talk or hybrid following README 4.1. "I'm presenting", "I'm giving the talk" or "live in front of an audience" means a talk deck with talk type sizes; offer a reading version for sending as an add-on. "For sending" or "send it as a PDF" means a reading deck. As a link for phones 32 to 40 px or a separate portrait version (H). German phrases: [markets/german.md](../markets/german.md) 1.
- **Situation:** the investor opens the link alone on a laptop or phone; 2:24 minutes for the whole deck at the end of 2023, longest on financials, team, competition (medium [5]). In the meeting a few seconds per slide.
- **Success:** after one pass the thesis, market, traction and team can each be repeated in one sentence; the reading version works without a speaker. Assertion titles with evidence improve understanding and retention (high [6]).
- **AI failure modes:** topic titles "Problem", "Market" (tell V16); problems as icon tiles (tell C8, D3); KPI cards and stat banners (tell D4, V4); hockey stick without an axis, cumulative curves, TAM, SAM, SOM as dot grids without a common scale (tell V17); violet gradient (tell B1); 29 type sizes in 11 slides (tell F4, V15; Reference: Gamma); abstract line or pure typography instead of a tangible idea; problem slide with a topic image instead of the problem; a title that says the negative first; the same key figure on more than two slides; "to be added" label (tell E6) or dashed boxes on many slides and notes under the company's own figures, which make the deck look like a review draft; product painted or composited into a foreign photo; the same image on more than two slides; only white slides, all of the same slide type.
- **Pros:** key sentences first, the title chain tells the deck; key figure with comparison, definition and absolute base (medium [7]); comparison as a color pair: status quo gray, the company's own product in brand color, same sentence form, reference directly under the figure, factors like "2x" only with a base (Reference: Duarte Thermo Fisher); no logo and decoration on every slide (medium [2]); same content type, same layout; team with real photos and relevant track record; one tangible idea from the product's world carries the deck, and one concrete case (a customer, a day, an object) runs as a through-line from problem through solution to benefit; if it is not a real customer, it is introduced as an example, and traction, market and ask carry only real figures from the brief; titles name the benefit and read positively on their own; the core property of the product from the brief stands on the title and solution slides in the title or anchor, not only in small print; every key figure at most twice (stage and closing).
- **Directions:** home DAT. R0 DAT (KPI cards, topic titles): near DAT in best form; edge EDI with material per the gate ([directions/README.md](../directions/README.md) 9: the company's own photos, client material or a public-domain archive whose subject is the subject of the brief), otherwise the next one in the ranking moves up; bold ONE only on title, chapter, vision and closing slides inside the base's system, never as the base of the proof slides ([ONE.md](../directions/ONE.md) 2: no data proof for investors). If the ranking would recommend ONE as the base, recommend the strongest direction with material (M from 2) and show ONE's idea as its accent ([directions/README.md](../directions/README.md) 12); if the user chooses ONE anyway, the proof slides keep the data forms of a base and the wrap-up names the loss. Excluded MAX, RAW. M-SPA as one moment for hardware, M-HUM as an accent.
- **Assets:** core thesis, key figures with period and source, team photos, product or material photos, market source; for hardware CAD data or renderings (they later carry the website and the trade fair). Substitute: best-form stand-in plus asset request (example Mängelwerk, software for construction-defect management, Series A: a free photo of a building shell with a marked defect as a situation image, in the review view "wanted: photo of the site manager with a tablet on your pilot site, 1600 x 900"); team without photos with a typographic anchor (name, role, track record), never stock faces; figures only from the brief, without a figure another anchor form. Without the company's own photos, with web access and unless the user chose no photos (Q5): after the answers and before the ranking, search public-domain archives once for the subject of the brief (two or three queries in museum, library and government collections, status per [images-and-assets.md](../images-and-assets.md) 3.6); a find that shows the subject itself is EDI material, and the queries and the result go into contract field 7.
- **Output:** HTML for the talk, PDF for sending, PPTX on request through a PPTX format skill. R-Slide.
- **Check:** read the title chain one below the other; every proof slide (result, traction, price, market, ask) shows its key figure from 160 px with its base, drawn where it is a comparison, a period or a whole, and none is a template stat slide; the closing slide repeats the thesis, three to four key figures and the ask ([craft.md](../craft.md) 12.2); glance test per slide at 25% (medium [8]); `generic-check.mjs` with `--deck reading` and `talk`, tell F4 and F6; slide types by eye; contact sheet of the deck: at least two full-bleed surfaces and one color ground, at most three open items in final form and none on stage slides, no image more than twice; every chart against the data rules; reading version at 390 px (a slide shrinks to about 360 x 203 px); in the hybrid: does the statement layer carry every slide alone?

## Case A3 Investor update and board deck

Who: S high, monthly to quarterly. Register Read. Evidence: update rule-based; investor and board deck Reference: Slidor Air France-KLM (studio self-report only, also a counterexample for data).

- **Specs:** update as an email (plain text or simple HTML) in a fixed order: summary, KPIs with the previous month, 3 to 5 highlights, 2 to 3 lowlights, 2 to 4 concrete asks, cash and runway, team (medium [9]). Board deck as a pre-read at least 48 hours ahead, questions to the board explicit, answer first (medium [10]).
- **Minimum type size:** email following web, board deck following reading deck.
- **Situation:** the investor reads on the phone between meetings in about two minutes (H); the board reads the pre-read thoroughly, often printed.
- **Success:** month-over-month comparison without follow-up questions; answers to the asks; the meeting starts with discussion instead of a presentation.
- **AI failure modes:** newsletter look with header graphic and emoji (tell C9); KPI tiles with green arrows (tell D4, P1); key figure without the previous month; curve without an axis; lowlights missing; percentages as freely scaled shapes (tell V17).
- **Pros:** the same KPI table in the same order every month; a sparkline per key figure with a gray normal band, minimum and maximum marked, or a bullet graph with gray tones of 40, 25, 10% black (medium [11]); definition at the figure; asks with names or a profile. Investor and results deck: chapters as a full-bleed image of the company's own object (plant, product, fleet), occasion and date in the edge strip, one title typeface, at most 8 sizes; replace category colors with a legend by one focus series with direct labels (Reference: Slidor Air France-KLM).
- **Directions:** home DAT. Starter set near DAT; no edge or bold option, the design steps back. Excluded STI, EDI, HER, WRM, DRW, ONE, MAX, TYP, RAW.
- **Assets:** KPI time series with definition, list of asks. Substitute: show a missing figure in final form in the KPI table (row with key figure and previous month, value field in the table's typography; [SKILL.md](../../SKILL.md) stage 4, point 6), listed in the review view, never estimated.
- **Output:** HTML email or plain text, charts as PNG with alt text; board deck as PDF and HTML. R-Mail, R-Slide.
- **Check:** view at 375 px; under 80 KB; every KPI with comparison and definition; order as in the previous month.

## Case B2 Website

Top 12, rank 10. Who: S high, B2B and B2C medium. Register Persuade. Evidence: measured clusters SaaS, premium, Germany and Europe (medium); counterexample Reference: Personio (generic index 5: tell A4, B1, C1, C3, cluster Glass look).

- **Specs:** page types home, product, pricing (case B4), about, careers, blog, contact, plus the legal pages the market requires (for example legal notice and privacy policy; [compliance.md](../compliance.md) 3; EU and DACH: [markets/eu-dach-compliance.md](../markets/eu-dach-compliance.md) 8); OG image per page type (case B3); favicon set favicon.ico 32, icon.svg with a dark variant, apple-touch-icon 180, manifest 192 and 512 (medium [12]); web values in README 4.2. Decision-critical sections are never missing (B2B: trust with privacy and data location, integration, contact person); open items in final form in the right place following case B1 ([b2b-marketing.md](b2b-marketing.md)), asked about in the kickoff.
- **Minimum type size:** web; input fields 16 px, otherwise iOS zooms.
- **Situation:** a prospect, applicant, investor or journalist checks within a few minutes whether the company really exists; 57% of viewing time above the fold, 74% in the first two screens (high [13]).
- **Success:** price, contact and product proof within at most two clicks; all page types share a recognizable system without looking the same.
- **AI failure modes:** every subpage as a copy of the landing page (tell D5); section sequence logos, three features, testimonials, pricing, FAQ, CTA (tell D9, V7); invented customer logos and testimonials (tell E5, V5, V6); missing legal pages; permanent dark without a scene reason (tell B5); mobile only stacked (tell V22); product only as an abstract diagram; trust section left out because details are missing.
- **Pros:** sitemap and page types first, then idea, through-line and material ([SKILL.md](../../SKILL.md) stage 4, points 1 and 2), then component inventory, content model, tokens; change composition instead of color (ground rule G13); mobile as its own edit (ground rule G11); DACH trust: supervision or data location by name, at most four curated seals, mandatory notices at reading size (medium; [markets/german.md](../markets/german.md) 7); one tangible idea from the product's world and one customer case as a through-line across all page types; the home page shows the offer (software as a mockup from the brief, a physical product in its situation); the headline names the benefit and reads positively on its own.
- **Directions:** B2B home ORD, PRO, RUL; R0 PRO: near ORD or PRO (only with a product, with a mandatory accent), edge RUL (chapters per product line), bold DRW (a character makes the intangible tangible); excluded MAX. B2C home STI, EDI, WRM, DRW; R0 WRM: near WRM or DRW in best form, edge STI or EDI, bold TYP or MAX; excluded DAT. M-MOT quiet, M-SPA at most as one moment.
- **Assets:** product UI or product photos, team photos, approved proof, legal details, wordmark. Substitute: product UI as a mockup from the features in the brief with the customer case as sample data ([images-and-assets.md](../images-and-assets.md) 5, sample data following 3.5), marked as a draft in the review view; a drawn diagram does not replace it. Proof only real, never invented and never as an empty box; without approved logos no logo strip, the trust section works with the material available ([compliance.md](../compliance.md) 3; EU and DACH: [markets/eu-dach-compliance.md](../markets/eu-dach-compliance.md) 10). Stand-in photos show the claim of the surface, otherwise a final-form slot; team page without generated people and without stock faces.
- **Output:** multi-page HTML, tokens as CSS variables, OG images per page type. R-Web.
- **Check:** R-Web per page template; contact sheet of all page types (system recognizable, rhythm varied); 10-second test of the first screen; longest word per display line at 390 px (German compounds: [markets/german.md](../markets/german.md) 3).

## Case B4 Pricing page and comparison table

Who: S high (SaaS), B2B and B2C medium. Register Persuade with Read, the table following Operate. Rule-based.

- **Specs:** 2 to 4 plans; comparison of at most 5 options, mobile 2; plans as columns, features as rows with labels on the left; header row with name, price and action fixed while scrolling, on mobile the first column too; highlight differences, identical rows can be hidden (high [14]). Price per unit plus a calculation for a typical scenario ("12 users: €348 per month", currency and number format of the market, [copy.md](../copy.md) 3), a range instead of only "on request" (high [15]); monthly and yearly switchable, with the savings as an amount in the market's currency; tabular figures, prices right-aligned.
- **Law:** consumer price display (total price, reference price for discounts), the B2B tax note legible next to the price, and order and cancellation buttons follow the market's rules ([compliance.md](../compliance.md) 3; EU and DACH, including the lowest price of the last 30 days next to a strikethrough price: [markets/eu-dach-compliance.md](../markets/eu-dach-compliance.md) 5 and 3; high [16]).
- **Minimum type size:** web; price, tax note and footnotes are must-read (12 px, target 14 px); table cells 14 to 16 px.
- **Situation:** comparison with purchase intent, often in several tabs against competitors; the decision-maker forwards it to purchasing (H).
- **Success:** after one look the user names the right plan and the monthly amount for their case.
- **AI failure modes:** three identical cards, the middle one with a violet gradient and "Most popular" (tell B1, C1, D3); checkmark lists without differences; "Save 20%" without an amount; invented prices and logos (tell E5, V5); FAQ template sequence (tell D9); emoji checkmarks (tell C9).
- **Pros:** recommendation by situation ("for teams of 5 or more") instead of "popular"; value metric visible (per user, per transaction) with a calculator; short view with differences only, full comparison below; enterprise with a range or scenario; what happens after the trial in one sentence.
- **Directions:** inherits the website's direction. Home ORD, DAT. R0 PRO for SaaS (WRM for a B2C app): near ORD or DAT; edge RUL (plan as primitive) or RAW (honest cost breakdown); bold none, the expression comes from the rest of the website. Excluded EDI, HER, TYP.
- **Assets:** real prices, units, features per plan, tax status of the audience. Substitute: an open price in final form in the table (price field in the table's typography; [SKILL.md](../../SKILL.md) stage 4, point 6), never as a label, underscore or bracket, listed in the review view, never invented and never the main figure. Question in the kickoff: "Do you sell to consumers, to businesses or to both?"
- **Output:** HTML. R-Web at 320, 390, 1440 px.
- **Check:** number of columns; fixed header row; scenario price; tax note readable; mobile table without overflow; tabular figures; recommendation at most once and reasoned.

## Case C7 App store screenshots

Who: S high (apps), B2C medium. Register Persuade. Rule-based; principle "state before feature" from Reference: Flighty.

- **Specs:** sizes in README 4.2. Apple: real app UI, one dark mode image if supported; without a preview video the first 1 to 3 images appear in search (high [17]). Google Play: no words like "Best", "#1", "Top", "New", "Discount", "Sale", "Million Downloads", no calls to action, feature graphic also without "Free" (high [18]). App icon 1024 x 1024 in default, dark, clear, tinted, no UI in the icon, recognizable at 64 px (high [19]).
- **Minimum type size:** caption readable at search size, start at 100 to 130 px at 1290 px width (H).
- **Situation:** search in the store, the first images small next to competitors, decision in seconds.
- **Success:** benefit understood in the first three images (high [17]).
- **AI failure modes:** invented UI that does not match the app (tell V1, V18); phone mockup with glow (tell C3); one template where only the color changes (tell V23); ranking claims; type too small for the search view.
- **Pros:** real screens from device or simulator; image 1 the core benefit as a short sentence plus real UI; the sequence tells a flow; caption as a benefit, not as a feature name; state before feature (time, place, status); localization per market (case X6).
- **Directions:** home PRO. R0 PRO as costume (phone with glow): near PRO in best form; edge WRM (UI fragment over an everyday photo) or RUL (category as primitive); bold MAX (lead color as area) or DRW (only with the brand's own character). Excluded HER, RAW.
- **Assets:** real screenshots of all device sizes, icon. Substitute: only a layout template, screens as a drawn flow sketch (never fake UI, never a gray box), marked as a template; do not deliver without real UI.
- **Output:** PNG in exact pixels without alpha; HTML template with variables per market. R-Raster.
- **Check:** file check (README 5) per file; contact sheet of the first three images at search size; text scan for the Google Play words; tagline under 20% of the area; dark mode image present.

## Case D3 Business stationery

Who: S high (first applications of an identity), B2B medium. Register Persuade and Read. Parts: letterhead first and following page, email signature; business card with print details in case I3 ([print-and-physical.md](print-and-physical.md)). Rule-based.

- **Specs letterhead:** A4 or US Letter by market; header, address field for the window envelope, margins, information block and fold and hole marks follow the market's letter standard (Germany: DIN 5008 form B with all measurements, medium [20], [markets/german.md](../markets/german.md) 7); date in the market's format ([copy.md](../copy.md) 3; German: [markets/german.md](../markets/german.md) 3); following page without header, with page number.
- **Mandatory notices** on all business letters, emails included, where the market requires them (company, legal form, registered office, register details, managing directors): [compliance.md](../compliance.md) 3; EU and DACH: [markets/eu-dach-compliance.md](../markets/eu-dach-compliance.md) 8 (high [21]).
- **Specs signature:** HTML text, never only an image; logo optional as PNG at double resolution with alt text; name, role, company, phone, web, mandatory notices; 8 to 10 lines, up to 600 px wide; system font (H).
- **Minimum type size:** letter following print, target 10 to 12 pt, note zone above the address (where the market's letter standard has one; Germany: [markets/german.md](../markets/german.md) 7) around 8 pt; signature following web, target 13 to 14 px.
- **Situation:** letter as a PDF by email or printed in a window envelope; the signature stands under thousands of emails, mostly read on phones (H).
- **Success:** the address sits in the window; mandatory notices complete; brand recognizable without disturbing the content.
- **AI failure modes:** color wave or bar at the edge without origin (tell V2, V14); huge logo (tell V19); icons before every contact line (tell C10); address outside the window; signature as an image; QR code without purpose; missing mandatory notices.
- **Pros:** one primitive from the logo orders all parts; emptiness as a value; the house typeface in the letter too, if the license allows office use, otherwise a documented system fallback ([fonts.md](../fonts.md)); the grid respects window and fold.
- **Directions:** home ORD, STI, RUL. R0 ORD (letterhead with a color bar): near ORD in best form with an own asset; edge RUL or STI; bold TYP, ONE or HER. Excluded PRO, DAT.
- **Assets:** logo as vector, house typeface with desktop license, register details, names and roles. Substitute: missing register details as a small, typographically embedded gap in the register line, listed in the review view, never invented.
- **Output:** DOTX or DOCX through a DOCX format skill, PDF; HTML signature. R-Document, R-Mail.
- **Check:** overlay of the market's address window (Germany: DIN 5008, [markets/german.md](../markets/german.md) 7) on the 794 x 1123 px view (US Letter: 816 x 1056 px, README 5); fold and hole marks; mandatory notices; signature at 375 px, without images and dark (dark type on a transparent PNG disappears).

## Case J2 Launch kit

Who: S high, B2B and B2C medium. Register Persuade and Feed. Rule-based; system principle from Reference: Next.js Conf and Reference: Figma Config (medium).

- **Specs:** Product Hunt thumbnail 240 x 240, gallery 1270 x 760, at least 2 images, video only as a YouTube link, description up to 260 characters (high [22]); OG image (case B3); social 1080 x 1350 and 1080 x 1920; landing page (case B1); email (case G1); press kit (case J3); for apps case C7.
- **Minimum type size:** per format following README 4; thumbnail at 240 px without text or with at most one word.
- **Situation:** a fixed launch date; all channels on the same day; the key visual appears in many sizes within a few days.
- **Success:** by the launch date all formats are complete, platform-compliant and recognizable as one brand, even without the logo.
- **AI failure modes:** every format reinvented (tell V15); gallery of phrases instead of the product (tell E1); glow blobs and shiny spheres as the key visual (tell C3, V1); formats missing on the launch date; product painted or composited into a foreign photo; all posts in the same base color; a where-to-buy surface that stalls ("more soon") or shows the way to buy as a "to be added" label (tell E6).
- **Pros:** asset list with sizes first; the key visual comes from a tangible idea of the product's world and shows the product in its situation; key message as a benefit, readable positively on its own; the core property of the product from the brief stands in the key message or key visual, not only in small print; where to buy, price and date always appear in the kit (at least on the landing page and the launch post); the where-to-buy surface (shop, retailers, locations, date) shows how to get it instead of stalling ("more soon"), open items on it in final form ([SKILL.md](../../SKILL.md) stage 4, point 6: list, card or field with a field label in the typography of the content, never a "to be added" label), none on the key visual, at most one per surface and three per sequence, never left out; one material for all formats (pixel, glyph set, product shape) with a connection to the audience; one layout per format, only variables and at most two accent colors are swapped (Reference: Figma Config); the posts of the launch series change ground or light and dark, the same image stands at most twice in full form; a key visual that carries from the 240 px thumbnail to the billboard; on sign-up a shareable personal image per participant, with consent (Reference: Next.js Conf); approval board with all files; motion goes to a video skill in the same system.
- **Directions:** home PRO (software), STI (hardware). R0 PRO: near PRO; edge STI (hardware; without an object photo ONE moves up, with the key visual as punchline); bold DRW (only with the brand's own character), MAX or TYP. No exclusion. M-SPA as one moment (product object), M-MOT for the launch film.
- **Assets:** product UI or product photos, logo, key message, date. Substitute: software as a mockup from the features in the brief with a running case, marked as a draft in the review view and never passed off as a real screenshot; a physical product as an illustration from its description; typography or a primitive (TYP, RUL) accompany the key visual, they do not carry it alone.
- **Output:** PNG set and HTML templates, folders by channel, file names `brand_channel_WIDTHxHEIGHT_variant.png`. R-Raster, R-Web.
- **Check:** contact sheet of all formats; every file against README 4.2; thumbnail at 240 px; the first gallery image tells the product.

## Case J3 Press kit

Who: S, B2B, B2C medium (funding, launch). Register Read. Rule-based.

- **Specs:** no standard. Usual: logo as SVG and PNG for light and dark grounds, high-resolution product images, founder or board photos, factsheet (case G4), company description, press release, contact; as the press area of the website.
- **Minimum type size:** factsheet following print, press area following web.
- **Situation:** a journalist under time pressure looks for a usable image and three facts within a minute (H).
- **Success:** usable images and facts within a minute. 72% of journalists surveyed name press releases the most useful PR resource; multimedia increases the effect (medium [23]).
- **AI failure modes:** marketing collage instead of free images; logo only as JPG; press photos with a filter or burned-in text; invented quotes from the board (tell E5).
- **Pros:** images usable editorially (without text, portrait and landscape per motif, caption and credit); data graphics for embedding; descriptive file names; license note; folders by use.
- **Directions:** follows the brand; frame home ORD. Starter set near ORD; edge RUL or STI; bold HER or EDI (only with an archive or photography respectively). Excluded MAX, TYP.
- **Assets:** press photos with approval, recognizable people only with consent ([compliance.md](../compliance.md) 3; EU and DACH: [markets/eu-dach-compliance.md](../markets/eu-dach-compliance.md) 9), logo files, facts. Substitute: a list of the missing files instead of substitute images; stand-ins never belong in the press kit, because editors use them as real.
- **Output:** folder or ZIP, HTML press area, PDF factsheet. R-Web, R-Document.
- **Check:** no image with burned-in text; logo variants on light and dark; file names and credits complete.
