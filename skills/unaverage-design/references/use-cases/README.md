# Use cases

As of 2026-10-08. Evidence strength: high (platform, norm, law, study), medium (secondary source, case study, measured reference), H (derivation). Sources [n] at the end.

## 1 How to read

- **When:** stage 0 the row in 2, then the profile; stage 5 the gates in 4 plus the profile's check items. Determine the case from prompt, format and place of use; ask only when it is ambiguous. For mixed jobs the key surface determines the case.
- **Groups:** S [startup.md](startup.md), B2B [b2b-marketing.md](b2b-marketing.md), B2C [b2c-marketing.md](b2c-marketing.md), P [product-design.md](product-design.md), BR [brand.md](brand.md), PP [print-and-physical.md](print-and-physical.md); case X6 in 7. Group rules apply to all profiles of their file.
- **Profile:** header (rank, who, register, evidence), Specs, Minimum type size, Situation, Success, AI failure modes with tell ID ([generic-catalog.md](../generic-catalog.md)), Pros, Directions, Assets with substitute, Output (5), Check beyond [review.md](../review.md).
- **Evidence:** "Reference: name" means an outstanding work was measured and supports the values; **rule-based** means only norms, platform rules and studies carry the profile.

## 6 Test plan for ads and social (mandatory output)

For cases E1, E2, E3, F1 to F4 and X2 as `test-plan.md` next to the images; reviewers do not replace a market test.

1. **Concepts:** 3 to 5 truly different ones per ad group (high [19]), different in message, angle or motif, not in color (medium [20]); at least two directions from the set. YouTube up to 3 variants, winner by watch time (high [12]).
2. **Per variant:** concept in one sentence (key sentence, anchor, direction); hypothesis "If we show X instead of Y, Z rises for Q, because ..."; what stays constant; metric (B2B lead quality before click rate; organic: saves, shares, swipe rate); file name.
3. **Naming:** `brand_channel_WIDTHxHEIGHT_c2-number_v1.png`; all formats of a variant carry the same concept part.
4. **Duration and threshold** are set by the user; without a threshold there is no winner (H).

## 7 Case X6 Localized variants

Cross-cutting, in addition to the profile when there is more than one language. Question: "Which languages, and which one is the longest?"

- **Length** compared with English: up to 10 characters 200 to 300%, 11 to 30 characters 160 to 200%, 31 to 50 characters 140 to 160%, over 70 characters 130% (high [21]); design with the longest language, copy as variables.
- **Gates:** `lang` per language, `dir="rtl"` for Arabic and Hebrew (high [22]); minimum type size per language, Arabic about 2 pt larger next to Latin caps; RTL mirrors layout, navigation and directional icons, never logos, photos, clocks or digits (high [23]).
- **Writing systems:** CJK and Hangul display line height from 1.4, no italics, `word-break: keep-all`; never letter-space Arabic; without word spaces count in characters and suspend tell D8 and F6 (high [24]; Reference: YAMAP, Reference: Baemin).
- **Formats** through `Intl` with the locale of each variant: en-US `October 8, 2026` and `$1,234.56`, en-GB `8 October 2026` and `£1,234.56` (German format: [markets/german.md](../markets/german.md) 3); check regulated color codes (stock-market red means rising in China and Korea).
- **Robustness** (H): high ORD, DAT, RUL, STI, PRO; medium WRM, EDI, DRW, MAX, RAW; low TYP, ONE, HER, then downgrade or build a separate version per language (Reference: Saudi Design Festival).
- **AI failure modes:** broken layout (tell H1, H2), a date in the format of the wrong locale (for example a US date outside the US), tofu boxes for missing glyphs, mirrored logos. **Check:** every language at 320, 390, 1440 px; if a native-speaker check is missing, list it as open.
