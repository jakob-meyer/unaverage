# B2B marketing

As of 2026-10-10. Matrix, gates, render paths and test plan in [README.md](README.md), ranking in [directions/README.md](../directions/README.md). Evidence strength high, medium, H (derivation); sources [n] at the end.

## Group rules

- **Proof architecture:** a result figure as the main message, a real customer with photo, the buyer's personal benefit (medium [1]), a forwardable summary for the buying group.
- **Different feelings, not fewer:** reassurance, competence, recognition, relief; emotional B2B campaigns more often work very strongly (medium [2]), hence one bold option from group IV or V in the set. Brand in the scene instead of a logo panel; the more intangible the product, the more tangible the image (medium [3]).
- **Data rules:** assertion titles, gray plus one accent, direct labels, unit, bars from zero, no dual axis, no cumulative curve as growth, pie at most 4 segments, at most 7 colors, source and date (medium [4]) for third-party figures, collected in a footnote or the appendix; the company's own statements without a note ([SKILL.md](../../SKILL.md) stage 4, point 4). Comparison as a color pair: status quo gray, product in brand color, same sentence form, reference under the figure (Reference: Duarte Thermo Fisher).
- **Decks (cases A2, A4, A5):** assertion titles; 3 to 7 slide types; at most 4 points per list, 2 lines per point (high [5]); accent word only when bound (always the same core term, or the statement in color and its classification in regular), otherwise tell A3 (Reference: Duarte Salesforce). Never justify with "award-winning". "I'm presenting", "I'm giving the talk" or "live in front of an audience" means a talk deck, "for sending" or "send it as a PDF" a reading deck (README 4.1; German phrases: [markets/german.md](../markets/german.md) 1); story slides as drafts, never placeholder titles.
- **Email (cases G1, G7):** live text for everything must-read, one column, tables with inline CSS; descriptive alt text, never "IMG" or empty for people (high [6]); at 375 px the headline is the largest line, at least 1.25 times the reading text (H).

## Case A2 Sales deck

Top 12, rank 8. Who: B2B, S high. Register Read and Distance, mostly hybrid. Evidence: Reference: Duarte Thermo Fisher (medium).

- **Specs:** 1920 x 1080. Dramaturgy following Raskin: shift, winners and losers, promised land, obstacles, evidence; the product comes in only late, as the way over the obstacles (medium [7]). Summary of 1 to 3 pages for forwarding; modules per industry or role. **Decision-critical slides are never missing:** comparison with the status quo and alternatives, implementation with timeline and integration, for software privacy and data location, team or contact person with track record, next step. The slide works with what is known, open items stand in final form on their working slide ([SKILL.md](../../SKILL.md) stage 4, point 6: card, row or field with a short field label such as "Liability" or "Implementation time" in the typography of the content, never a "to be added" label or dashed box), none on stage slides (title, solution, strongest figure, next step), at most one per slide and three in the deck, everything else only in the review view and the wrap-up; if something is open at the next step (for example a date), the slide works with what is known and the open part stands only in the review view and the wrap-up; asked about in the kickoff; never leave it out and never evade ("we'll clarify that in the meeting"). The company's own statements stand without a note, sources only for third-party figures, collected in a footnote or the appendix.
- **Minimum type size:** hybrid deck (README 4.1): title 56 to 68 px, key figure 150 to 190 px and a context line from 40 px carry the slide alone; callouts and axes from 22 px.
- **Situation:** a meeting with two to six people, then forwarding by the champion (H). **Success:** the customer recognizes their situation within three slides; forwarding works without sales; next step agreed.
- **AI failure modes:** feature card grid (tell D3, C8); product on slide 2; isometric stock illustration (tell V14); buzzwords (tell E1); invented logos (tell E5, V5); a colored keyword in every title (tell A3); product only as an abstract diagram; a title that says the negative first; "to be added" label (tell E6) or dashed boxes on many slides; only white slides, all of the same slide type.
- **Pros:** slide library per segment; one tangible idea from the customer's working day carries the deck (for example the consignment note of a freight forwarder), one customer case runs as a through-line from the obstacles to the result; problem slides show the problem in the image, not the topic; per feature slide a real screenshot in the customer context, without a screenshot a mockup from the features in the brief with this case as sample data; titles name the benefit and read positively on their own; the core property of the product from the brief stands on the solution slide in the title or anchor; proof slide with logo, figure and named quote; rhythm following [craft.md](../craft.md) 5.5, even in calm directions at least two full-bleed surfaces (color from the brand world or a customer image bleeding off the edge) and one color ground, an image at most twice in full form. Products without an interface get a component slide: rendering on 55 to 75% of the area, cropped, at most 6 callouts with hairlines; dark for the object, light for data, always the same.
- **Directions:** home PRO, DAT. R0 DAT: near PRO (only with a product) or DAT; edge RUL (chapter color per product line) or EDI (customer photos); bold none, ONE only for title and closing. Excluded MAX, TYP, RAW.
- **Assets:** product UI, CAD or renderings, approved logos, quotes, figures. Substitute: product UI as a mockup from the brief (Pros), marked as a draft in the review view; the proof slide stays and works with a stand-in anchor and the material available; open logos and figures never invented, without approval no logo strip, the request stands in the review view ([compliance.md](../compliance.md) 3; EU and DACH: [markets/eu-dach-compliance.md](../markets/eu-dach-compliance.md) 10).
- **Output:** PPTX in the template's placeholders plus PDF, HTML for a live demo. R-Slide.
- **Check:** as case A1 ([startup.md](startup.md)); summary understandable on its own; PPTX placeholders in PowerPoint.

- **Assets:** company template, data; without a template a neutral ORD template (case J4).

- **Check:** title chain; placeholders in PowerPoint; for a correction before and after as a contact sheet.

- **Assets:** product UI or product photos, approved quote, brand color, wordmark. Substitute: product UI as a mockup from the features in the brief with the running case as sample data ([images-and-assets.md](../images-and-assets.md) 5, sample data following 3.5), marked as a draft in the review view; a flow sketch does not replace it. Proof only real; without approved logos no logo strip, every decision-critical section works with the material available and at most one open item in final form ([compliance.md](../compliance.md) 3; EU and DACH: [markets/eu-dach-compliance.md](../markets/eu-dach-compliance.md) 10).
