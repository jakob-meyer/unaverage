This repository is the marketplace of the unaverage skills and holds one skill, `skills/unaverage-design`. It is written in English and works in English and German; rules for specific markets, such as German copy and typography or EU and DACH law, live in modules in its `references/markets/` folder.

## Origin

unaverage-design comes from the author's own research and testing. Before its first public release it was called design-craft; the versions below carry that name.

- Version 1 (October 5, 2026) started from the analysis of a publicly shared comparison of two pitch decks with identical content, one generated by AI in a single pass and one designed by a professional.
- Version 2 (October 2026) rests on a dedicated research phase: design history, studios, product and web design, marketing effect, presentation and information design, generic patterns in AI output, craft and resources, process and quality, use cases, law and accessibility, plus about 70 reference works that were rendered and measured. Sources are listed at the end of the files in the skill's `references/` folder.
- Version 2.5.2, still written in German, combines the generative core of version 1 with the knowledge of version 2; its predecessors were refined over six rounds of internal blind tests. Method and limits of these tests are described in [docs/testing.md](docs/testing.md).
- The published skill is written in English and adapted from version 2.5.2.

## What the skill contains and what it does not

- The skill takes only abstract principles and measured values from its references. It contains no designs, texts or images from third-party works.
- Names of studios, brands and works appear only as evidence, never as templates. Signatures of living studios and artists are not reproduced.
- Fonts are not included. The font catalog lists only freely licensed typefaces, with license and source for each one, in the skill's `references/` folder.
- The accents in a hand-drawn style in `assets/doodles/` (SVG) were created specifically for this skill.

## Third-party software

- The scripts in the skill's `scripts/` folder depend on `puppeteer-core` (Apache-2.0) and its dependencies (MIT, ISC, BSD-3-Clause, Apache-2.0). They are not included in this repository. The skill's `package.json` and `package-lock.json` pin the versions; `npm install` in the skill folder fetches them.
- Rendering and measuring use a locally installed Chrome, Chromium, Edge or Brave. No browser is included or downloaded.
- The Python image scripts use Pillow and NumPy, which are installed separately.
